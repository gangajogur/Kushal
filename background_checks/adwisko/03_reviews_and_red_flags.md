# 03 - Reviews and Red Flags

Independent feedback is thin; the company is about 3 years old and small. Treat all of it as weak.

## Positive
- **Aggregator listing (exa.ai place data, Hyderabad Somajiguda office): 5.0/5 from 44 reviews**, Sep-Dec 2025 snippets. Praise for named advisors (Zoya Shaikh, Harini, Paul Samuel, Jyoti Das, Sirac Damian), patience, honesty, SOP/editorial help; one user reports a US admit with scholarship; one Bachelor's applicant praised help with scholarships. Two reviewers did not proceed but rated well on the first conversation. Perfect average and a cluster of reviews in a few weeks could mean solicited reviews. Source platform is likely Google Maps but not confirmed.
- **Reddit (r/Indians_StudyAbroad, Apr 2026; r/studyAbroad, Jan 2026)**: user "SuspiciousRope62" says Adwisko is "pretty decent, more structured and honest" and that a friend is using it free for Ireland. **The same account appears in two threads, in both cases mentioning "a friend" going through Adwisko and, in one, intending to use them too.** This pattern looks promotional; not proof.
- Homepage testimonials (company-selected).

## Negative / cautionary
- **LinkedIn post by Keerthibhushan V (2026)**: after admission to MSc Computer Science at University College Dublin, asked Adwisko about scholarship/funding and was told **support was not available because the application had not been processed through their platform**. Also criticised "student-first" and government-linked narratives without clear upfront conditions. Single account.
- **Reddit r/Indians_StudyAbroad (Dec 2025)**: OP asked if Adwisko is genuine; one commenter says Adwisko told them "they will surely get me a scholarship" and asks if it is genuine (0 points, unsure). That is a guarantee-style promise made on a sales call, contrary to the FAQ. Other commenters give a general warning about commission-driven consultancies: registration fees, immediate dismissal of your qualifications, steering to tie-up universities that pay high commission, "guaranteed admission" to weak institutions, and that no consultancy can get admission. Not specific to Adwisko, but "guaranteed admission" wording is exactly the warning sign they describe.
- **Reddit r/studyAbroad (Jan-Feb 2026)** general thread: agents recommend "scholarships" for degree mills in Germany; commenters advise checking accreditation (ICEF QEA list) and avoiding for-profit universities. I found no ICEF or similar accreditation for Adwisko.
- No Glassdoor or AmbitionBox listing found; no consumer-court or RBI records checked.

## Structural red flags (from their own materials)
1. **"Guaranteed admission" + "guaranteed funding" on the About page** while the FAQ says no scholarship guarantee. Inconsistent and promotional.
2. **Screening-out of "low-probability" profiles** then quoting a 93.5% success rate.
3. **"Free" and "no commissions"** with no revenue explanation.
4. **Application fees paid to the company via UPI/NEFT, non-refundable, "study abroad related service fee" wording.**
5. **Common ownership with a loan aggregator** and a "partner" label that hides it.
6. **Government-support language** copied from WMS, unverified.
7. **"A decade of experience"** from a 2023 company.
8. **Tiny paid-up capital (INR 1 lakh)** behind guarantees.
9. **Scholarship eligibility conditioned on using the platform** (Dublin case) - consistent with a scholarship-as-funnel design.
10. **Possible astroturfing pattern** in forum mentions (one account across two threads).

## Fair counterpoints
- Many Indian consultancies charge INR 50K-2 lakh+; a genuinely free application-helper is useful for people who struggle with forms and SOPs.
- Individual advisors seem to receive consistent praise for responsiveness.
- Nothing found alleging money lost to Adwisko, an outright scam, or forged admissions.

## Net reading
Probably a real, small, sales-driven funnel for the WMS group. Risk is less "scam" and more: **being steered** (universities, scholarships, loan) and **overstating success** in marketing. The family should use it, if at all, as a free helper for forms and SOP and verify everything on university and government sites.
