## Relationship to WeMakeScholars
- Adwisko's About page: "started by a passionate team that created WeMakeScholars". Same two founders on the board of both companies.
- Adwisko FAQ: "We have partnered with WeMakeScholars to help all our students find the best education loan... WeMakeScholars will not be charging any service fee from Adwisko students."
- Homepage tagline: "supported by the WeMakeScholars Foundation, the CSR arm of WeMakeScholars - an initiative funded and supported by Govt. of India". I found no evidence of a registered "WeMakeScholars Foundation" (no trust/Section 8 registration in search results). WMS's university pages do use the name for co-branded loan-linked fee waivers (see 02).
- It is therefore a **sister company / lead-generation front-end** for the WMS group, not an independent partner. The word "partner" in the FAQ understates common ownership. Neither site says plainly that they share owners (the common directors are visible on MCA, not on the sites).
