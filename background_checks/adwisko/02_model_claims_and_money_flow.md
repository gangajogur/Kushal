# 02 - Model, Claims and Money Flow

## What they offer (their description)
- Admission help: shortlist from "1 lakh+ courses", dedicated Academic Advisor, application management, SOP support from an "Editorial team", guidance on at most ~8 courses.
- Scholarship help: an "AI-powered scholarship search engine" matches profile to scholarships; a Scholarship Advisor applies on the student's behalf to **5 scholarships per university**. Claims 3,200-3,300+ providers, "$5 Billion+ available scholarships", "~$10,000,000 scholarships" won for students.
- "Everything else": education loan via WeMakeScholars, forex deals, accommodation deals.
- Destinations: "all major study abroad destinations", no limit.
- 100% digital process.

## How does it earn? (not disclosed)
Adwisko says: "100% free", "No commissions. No pressure." (homepage, LinkedIn Jan 2026). No page explains who pays. Candidate revenue lines (hypotheses, none confirmed):
1. **University / agent commissions** - standard in the sector; would contradict "no commissions".
2. **Loan referral commission via WeMakeScholars** - WMS is paid by lenders on disbursal; an Adwisko student who borrows generates that revenue inside the group.
3. **Forex and accommodation referral deals** - listed on the homepage ("Deals for the lowest Forex rates...").
4. **Application-fee handling** - T&C allow Adwisko to collect university application payments; any margin is unknown.
5. **Group cross-subsidy from WMS** (profitable, ~INR 9.4 Cr PAT FY25 per `../wemakescholars/01`).
Ask which of these applies. A consultancy cannot be both "free, no commissions" and sustainable with 30+ advisors unless one of these (probably 1-3) exists.

## Terms that matter (adwisko.com/site/terms-and-conditions)
- Site is "owned and operated by Beca Scholarship Pvt Ltd".
- "You may use this website to make the payment for your university applications... we do not have a payment gateway integrated... UPI / NEFT / RTGS / IMPS." Disputes are handled by the bank/UPI platform; "Adwisko will not have any role".
- **Refund policy:** "Adwisko will not be able to refund any amount collected for third party payments such as but not limited to, university application fee or any study abroad related service fee."
- Implication: money for application fees may sit with Beca Scholarship Pvt Ltd (a INR 1 lakh capital entity) before reaching the university, and "study abroad related service fee" is open-ended wording that conflicts with "100% free". Pay universities directly through their own portals (check each university's application page; many take direct online payment, and some charge no application fee, but I did not verify this for the shortlist).

## The WMS "Foundation" scholarship mechanism (context for "scholarship" claims)
WMS university pages advertise "[University] in association with WeMakeScholars Foundation" awards, for example:
- **Jonkoping University** (Sweden): 30% tuition waiver for bachelor/master; needs admission, ~60% GPA, and "arrange the remaining funds through an Education Loan approved by WeMakeScholars".
- **Duke Law (LLM)**: USD 20,000 for two students; requires a loan approval through WMS.
So some WMS-group "scholarships" are **conditional on borrowing via WMS**. Combined with Adwisko applying to "5 scholarships per university", a student may be funnelled to awards whose real price is a WMS-arranged loan. Not confirmed that Adwisko uses these, but ask for the terms of each award it applies to, and whether any require a loan.
