## Questions to ask in writing before any commitment
1. Who owns Adwisko? Confirm it is the same owners as WeMakeScholars.
2. **How do you earn money?** Do you receive any payment from universities, scholarship bodies, lenders, forex or accommodation partners? How much per student?
3. Are you an official/authorised representative of QUT / Deakin / UWA (names the universities where you hold agreements)? Will you tell us which of our shortlist you have tie-ups with?
4. What does "guaranteed admission" mean exactly? In writing, what is guaranteed and what happens if it fails?
5. Provide the **terms of every scholarship** you plan to apply for: amount, source, deadline, and whether any require a loan from a specific lender (esp. WeMakeScholars).
6. Define the "93.5%" and "98.5%" metrics: denominator, period, how many students were rejected at intake, median scholarship value.
7. **What fees (if any) will we pay you, and for what?** Will we pay application fees to you or to the university? Refund terms for each.
8. Will you submit applications under our own university portal account, with our login retained by us?
9. Can we apply to the same university directly at the same time? (Confirm no exclusivity, given the Dublin complaint.)
10. Data: what is shared with WMS and lenders, and with consent for which purposes?

## Safe-use rules
- Create university accounts yourself; keep the passwords; pay application fees **directly to the university**.
- Do not accept a "guaranteed admission" to any university the family has not independently checked (ranking, accreditation, graduate outcomes, course accreditation like Engineers Australia / ACS for IT degrees).
- Do not take any loan solely to unlock a scholarship; compare with a direct SBI/public bank quote (see `../wemakescholars/05`).
- Cross-check scholarship claims on the university's own scholarship page.
- Keep records of chats and calls (given the platform-exclusivity complaint).
- Do not treat government-support language as a trust signal.
