## One-paragraph summary
Adwisko is an **admissions + scholarship-application consultancy for Indian students**, run by **Beca Scholarship Pvt Ltd** (CIN U85500TS2023PTC176723, incorporated 4 Sep 2023, Hyderabad, paid-up capital only INR 1 lakh). Its only two directors are the **WeMakeScholars founders, Damini Mahajan and Arjun Radha Krishna**; WeMakeScholars is named on the site as its "partner" for education loans. It is a young, very small entity (LinkedIn: 1-10 employees; site claims 30+ scholarship advisors) with almost no independent track record: Reddit feedback is thin and partly looks promotional, Google-style reviews are all 4-5 star (44 reviews on one aggregator), and I found only one detailed negative report (a student told scholarship help was unavailable because the application had not gone through Adwisko). Marketing is aggressive: "guaranteed admission", "guaranteed funding", "100% free", "no commissions", "93.5% win a scholarship", while its own FAQ says it does not guarantee scholarships and screens out low-probability profiles. How it earns money is **not disclosed**.

## Bottom line (for decision use)
1. **Not a stand-alone neutral party.** Same owners as WeMakeScholars. Expect the "funding" step to route into a WMS loan. Everything in `../wemakescholars/` (commission conflict, NBFC steering, processing-fee pressure) applies on top.
2. **"Free" does not mean no conflict of interest.** Adwisko says no commissions, but it must earn from somewhere (university partner commissions and/or WMS loan referrals are the obvious candidates; unconfirmed). Ask in writing.
3. **"Guaranteed admission" is a red flag.** Genuine selective universities do not guarantee admission via an agent. It implies steering to universities with easy entry or agent tie-ups.
4. **"Scholarship" may mean a small or loan-linked award.** WMS co-markets "WeMakeScholars Foundation" fee waivers with universities that require the student to take a WMS-approved loan (example: Jonkoping University 30% fee waiver). I could not confirm that Adwisko's scholarships are these, but check each award's conditions.
5. **Pay application fees directly to the university.** Adwisko's terms let it collect application payments by UPI/NEFT (no payment gateway) and say third-party and "study abroad related service" fees are non-refundable.
6. **Statistics are unaudited and self-selected.** The 93.5% / 98.5% figures exclude students Adwisko turns away and say nothing about scholarship size.
7. For the nephew (Bachelor's; QUT/Deakin, India-first), **Adwisko is not on QUT's official India representative list**, and QUT requires Indian applicants to use one (see `../qut_india_agents/`), so it cannot lodge a QUT application. See `04`.
