# 04 - Acronyms and Plain Terms

Glossary for this Credila check and for talking to lenders / aggregators about Kushal’s loan.

## Acronyms
| Acronym | Full form | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **NBFC** | Non-Banking Financial Company | Lender regulated by RBI, but not a bank. Cannot take public deposits like SBI; funds itself from markets/banks. Credila is an NBFC. |
| **RBI** | Reserve Bank of India | India’s central bank / financial regulator. Sets rules Credila must follow. |
| **EMI** | Equated Monthly Instalment | Fixed monthly repayment after the study “holiday” ends — principal + interest combined. |
| **SI** | Simple Interest | During study, you pay the interest that accrues that period. If paid in full, principal does not grow from unpaid interest. |
| **PSI** | Partial Simple Interest | You pay only *part* of the monthly interest during study. Unpaid interest is usually **added to the loan** (capitalised), so the balance can grow before EMIs start. |
| **CBLR** | Credila Benchmark Lending Rate | Credila’s internal base rate. Your rate ≈ CBLR + a “spread” (extra % based on risk). When CBLR moves, floating loans move. |
| **GST** | Goods and Services Tax | Tax on fees (e.g. processing fee). Often ~18% on top of the fee. |
| **CIBIL** | Credit Information Bureau (India) Limited | Main Indian credit score/report brand. A “hard pull” can temporarily dent the co-applicant’s score. |
| **RM** | Relationship Manager | Assigned Credila contact for docs, sanction, and disbursement. |
| **SLA** | Service Level Agreement | Promised turnaround (e.g. “disbursement within X working days of complete docs”). |
| **UG** | Undergraduate | Bachelor’s degree (Kushal’s case). **PG** = postgraduate / Master’s. |
| **GS** | Genuine Student | Australia student-visa assessment that the applicant is a genuine student (SOP, funds, ties, course fit). Loan sanction letters often support the funds part. |
| **GTE** | Genuine Temporary Entrant | Older name for a similar visa test; still heard informally. Same idea: prove study intent + funds. |
| **SOP** | Statement of Purpose | Written explanation of why this course / uni / country — used for uni and often visa. |
| **SBI** | State Bank of India | Large public bank; often cheaper education loans than NBFCs if you qualify. |
| **BoB** | Bank of Baroda | Another public bank commonly compared for education loans. |
| **WMS** | WeMakeScholars | Loan aggregator/facilitator; paid by lenders — incentive to get a loan closed. See `../wemakescholars/`. |
| **CA** | Chartered Accountant | Tax professional — needed to confirm interest deduction rules. |
| **ITR** | Income Tax Return | Annual tax filing used to prove co-applicant income. |
| **KYC** | Know Your Customer | Identity/address verification (PAN, Aadhaar, passport, etc.). |
| **PAN** | Permanent Account Number | Indian tax ID. |
| **AUD** | Australian dollar | Currency of Australian tuition/living costs. |
| **PE** | Private Equity | Investment firms (EQT, ChrysCapital) that now mostly own Credila. |
| **NPA** | Non-Performing Asset | Loans in serious default; health metric in rating reports. |
| **AUM** | Assets Under Management | Total outstanding loan book. |
| **ROI** | Return on Investment | Informal lender shorthand for “will this course lead to a job that can repay?” |
| **Sec 80E / Sec 129** | Income-tax sections | Old **80E** / newer labelling **129**: interest on education loans may be deductible **only under the old tax regime**. Confirm with a CA for an NBFC loan. |

## Other plain terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Floating rate** | Can go up/down after you take the loan (tied to CBLR / policy). |
| **Fixed rate** | Locked for a period; Credila usually prices fixed *above* floating. |
| **Sanction** | Formal approval letter with amount, rate, conditions. |
| **Disbursement** | Money actually sent (usually to the university). |
| **Collateral / secured** | Property / FD / similar pledged as security. |
| **Unsecured** | No property pledged; relies on co-applicant income/credit + student/course profile. |
| **Co-applicant** | Usually a parent who shares liability; their income/CIBIL drive approval. |
| **Moratorium** | Study period (+ sometimes grace) before full EMI; with Credila you usually still pay SI/PSI. |
| **Capitalisation** | Unpaid interest added to principal; you later pay interest on that interest. |
| **Origination / processing fee** | Upfront fee for processing (Credila advertises up to 1.25% + GST). |
| **Foreclosure / prepayment** | Paying off early or in lumps; Credila advertises **no** extra charge — confirm in writing. |
| **Top-up** | Extra loan later if fees / FX rise. |
| **Tranche** | Loan paid in parts (year 1, 2, 3) matching fee invoices. |
| **Hard credit pull** | Full credit enquiry that appears on the credit report. |
| **Soft credit pull** | Eligibility-style check that typically does not (or less severely) affect the score — confirm with lender. |
| **Key Facts Statement** | Plain summary of rate, fees, and key terms — insist on something equivalent in writing before paying fees. |
