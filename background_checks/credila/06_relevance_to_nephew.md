## Fit by path

### Australia (QUT / Deakin / UWA) — ~₹1.5–1.8 cr all-in
- Credila is a **realistic NBFC path** when a public bank is slow or collateral rules are awkward.
- Expect **mandatory co-applicant**, likely **collateral** at this ticket size (unsecured caps often ~₹1 cr and profile-driven), **floating rate**, and **SI/PSI during the 3-year course**.
- Family repayment models already show heavy EMI if he stays in Brisbane; return-to-India on a metro salary does not clear a QUT-sized EMI (`../../Loan/QUT_return_to_India_scenario.md`). Loan *terms* (rate, PSI, insurance) matter, but **loan size vs destination** is the bigger decision.
- Compare: Credila direct vs Credila via WMS vs **SBI / BoB** on the same cost sheet.

### APU Kuala Lumpur — ~₹0.41–0.56 cr
- Ticket size is more likely **unsecured-eligible** if co-applicant income/credit is strong.
- Credila’s rate premium vs a public bank may not be worth it for a smaller loan — still get both quotes.
- EMI in family models is far more survivable on an India salary than a QUT-sized loan (`../../Loan/APU_return_to_India_scenario.md`).

### India first
- Credila also does domestic education loans, but public-bank / campus schemes may be simpler and cheaper for Indian UG. Not the main reason to engage Credila unless a specific private-college package needs NBFC speed/amount.

## What Credila does *not* solve
- Does not replace the **Australia vs APU vs India** strategic call.
- Does not replace a **QUT-approved agent** for lodging (Credila is a lender, not an education agent) — see `../qut_india_agents/`.
- Does not remove co-applicant dependency; Kushal has no income.
