## History and ownership
- **2006:** Incorporated 1 Feb 2006; promoted by Anil Bohora and Ajay Bohora. Specialised education lender from the start. [O/3P ratings]
- **FY2020 / Dec 2019:** HDFC buys out promoters; Credila becomes wholly owned HDFC subsidiary. [CRISIL]
- **March 2024:** EQT and ChrysCapital acquire control from HDFC Bank (transaction reported ~₹9,500 cr). Company renamed Credila Financial Services Limited. [3P / CARE]
- **Through 9MFY25 / into 2026:** Shinhan Bank (~10%) and HDFC Life (~1.8%) take stakes; further capital infusions. [CARE]
- **As on 31 Mar 2026 (CRISIL / unlisted-share summaries):** roughly **EQT 64%**, **ChrysCapital entities 16%**, **Shinhan Bank 10.1%**, **HDFC Bank 8.2%**, **HDFC Life 1.8%**.

Implication: brand familiarity with “HDFC” remains, but majority economic control is private-equity-led. Do not assume HDFC Bank branch service or policies.

## Scale (from rating agencies; approximate)
- Large education-loan franchise; overseas focus; significant unsecured share.
- Branch / office network described as tens of branches plus back-office (figures vary by report date: e.g. ~32 branches end-2024 in one CARE note; CRISIL cites eight full-service offices + wider branch network).
- Sourcing channels: education consultants, digital, college/test-prep partners, some bank channels, direct marketing. [CRISIL]
- Portfolio note (CARE ~2026): ~**77% of AUM** under principal repayment moratorium; borrowers typically pay **partial interest**; remaining interest **capitalised**. Missed partial-interest payments treated as default for monitoring.
