## Secured vs unsecured
- **Secured:** collateral such as residential property, FDs, or insurance policies; legal/technical valuation; often better rate / higher amount.
- **Unsecured:** no property pledge; heavier weight on academics, institution, course employability, and **co-applicant income/CIBIL**.

## Study-period repayment (SI / PSI)
Not a full “pay nothing until after graduation” product for most borrowers:
- **SI (Simple Interest):** pay full monthly interest during study so principal does not grow from unpaid interest.
- **PSI (Partial Simple Interest):** pay a fixed partial amount; unpaid interest is typically **capitalised** into the outstanding balance.
- CARE: partial interest during moratorium is **strictly enforced**; missed payment can be classified as default for monitoring / recourse.
- After course (+ any grace period), full **EMI** (principal + interest) begins.

Ask for a sample capitalisation schedule before accepting PSI.

## Interest-rate model (company policy PDF)
Credila publishes an Interest Rate Policy describing a **Credila Benchmark Lending Rate (CBLR)** built from cost of debt, liquidity negative carry, operating cost, base risk premium, and portfolio margin. Individual loans = CBLR + risk/spread factors. Floating resets matter as much as the day-1 headline rate.

## Tax note (company site disclaimer)
Deduction under **Section 129** of the Income Tax Act, 2025 (erstwhile **Section 80E**) is described as available **only under the old tax regime**, not the new regime. Confirm with a CA whether interest on this **NBFC** loan qualifies and for whom (student vs co-applicant).

## Australia-specific (company Australia page)
- Age 16+; co-applicant generally required; bachelor’s and master’s; recognised Australian institutions.
- Marketing: up to 100% finance; tenure up to 15 years; rates “from 9.95%* onwards.”
- GyanDhan (Apr 2026, competitor/aggregator content): Australia/NZ starting rates often described around **~10.5%+**, with higher co-applicant income expectations in some cases — treat as 3P, verify with Credila.

## Documents (high level)
Student: KYC, academics, admission/fee letter.
