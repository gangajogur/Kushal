## One-paragraph summary
**Credila Financial Services Limited** (formerly HDFC Credila) is an RBI-registered, education-loan-only **NBFC**. Founded 2006; wholly owned by HDFC from late 2019; majority sold in March 2024 to **EQT + ChrysCapital** (~₹9,500 cr). As of 31 Mar 2026 roughly: EQT ~64%, ChrysCapital ~16%, Shinhan Bank ~10.1%, HDFC Bank ~8.2%, HDFC Life ~1.8%. It still markets under the Credila brand (many people still say “HDFC Credila”) but is **not** a majority-HDFC product and is **not a bank**. Public site: floating rates from **9.95% secured / 11.25% unsecured**, origination up to **1.25% + GST**, no advertised prepay/foreclosure charges, co-applicant mandatory, up to ~100% of education costs. During study it typically requires **SI or PSI**; unpaid interest is capitalised. Ratings agencies describe a large overseas book with low NPAs and a high share of loans still under principal moratorium. User feedback is mixed: faster than many public banks when docs are complete, but recurring pain around insurance bundling, floating-rate rises, processing delays, and late subsequent disbursements.

## Bottom line (for decision use)
1. **Legitimate specialised lender** — usable, especially for large overseas amounts or when a public bank is slow / collateral rules are awkward. Treat as a commercial NBFC, not “HDFC Bank service.”
2. **“Starting from” rates are floors.** Australia UG / weaker co-applicant profiles often land ~10.5–12%+ floating in third-party guides and borrower reports. Get a **written sanction** with CBLR + spread + reset frequency.
3. **For Kushal’s Australia ~₹1.5 cr ticket, expect collateral** (unsecured caps are often ~₹1 cr and profile-driven). APU-sized loans are more likely unsecured-eligible if co-applicant income/credit is strong — and a public bank may still win on rate.
4. **Budget SI/PSI for the whole Bachelor’s.** Credila is not a full zero-payment study holiday. Missed PSI is treated seriously (CARE notes partial interest is strictly enforced).
5. **Push back on insurance** — get mandatory vs optional in writing; ask whether premium is added to principal.
6. **Compare parallel quotes:** Credila direct vs same offer via WMS/aggregator vs SBI / Bank of Baroda (Vidya Lakshmi). Do not pay processing fees until terms are accepted in writing.
7. Use `05_questions_to_ask.md` as the email checklist before signing.
