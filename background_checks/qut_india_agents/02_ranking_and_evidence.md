# 02 - Ranking and Evidence

## How I ranked (transparent criteria)
There is no official or objective ranking of QUT agents. I ranked by:
1. Listed by QUT for India with an office in Pune/Mumbai/Nagpur/Hyderabad (all listed below pass).
2. Breadth of Australian-university partnerships (The PIE News analysis of published agent lists; undated).
3. Accountability: listed company, formal grievance route, industry certification (AIRC, ICEF QEAC), Australian presence.
4. Public feedback (Trustpilot, Reddit, Google summaries). Weak and self-selected.
5. Conflict-of-interest factors.
Not weighted: price (services are free to students at all of these per their sites; confirm in writing).
Reminder from users in every thread: you are a lead sold to whichever university pays; the individual counsellor drives outcomes.

## 1. IDP India
- Evidence: ASX-listed; "52 offices" retained across India after consolidating 15; claims Google 4.8; free counselling; partners with all 40 Australian universities that publish agent lists (PIE).
- FY26: revenue AUD 795.4M (-9%), student placements -27%, ~1,250 roles cut (20%), IELTS venues cut from ~1,500 to <600; outlook assumes volumes 20-30% lower. Australian visa fee A$2,000 hit placements by about a fifth (Class Central).
- Trustpilot (105 reviews, AI summary): praise for step-by-step guidance and visa support; complaints on delayed responses, lost documents, pressure towards "expensive course with high ROI" and their listed universities, one counsellor-conduct complaint (North Delhi). Reddit: "my agent barely replies" (Nov 2025).
- Conflict: IDP co-owns IELTS and earns on tests and placements.
- Why #1: scale, accountability and QUT presence in every nearby city; weakness is service variability and ongoing downsizing (ask which office will actually handle him; Solapur applicants will be routed to Pune/Mumbai).
- Sources: https://www.idp.com/india/ ; https://thepienews.com/idp-to-consolidate-15-india-student-placement-locations/ ; https://www.trustpilot.com/review/idp.com ; https://www.classcentral.com/report/idp-study-abroad/

## 2. AECC Global
- Evidence: Melbourne HQ (offices Brisbane, Sydney); "1M+ students, 1,100+ institution partners, 18+ years" (company); 34 Australian university partners (PIE); **AIRC certification period "through 2033"** (AIRC India list); QEAC-certified counsellors and in-house migration agents (company).
- Feedback: Reddit threads mostly positive about Australia (free, smooth visa, scholarships), one saying it "is not a scam" and likely earns from university partnerships. Trustpilot has serious complaints (rudeness, IELTS coaching refunds at CG Road Ahmedabad, "fake company" reviews, visa rejection). Worldorgs/Google-style Vijayawada review page mixed (some "visa refusal due to consultancy").
- Reddit user (Nov 2025) says counsellors "are basically salespeople with monthly targets" in both IDP and AECC.
- Why #2: certification and Australian base; its Brisbane office could be useful to you in Brisbane for the family-side questions (not required). Reviews do not separate it from IDP.
- Sources: https://www.aeccglobal.com/au/student-services ; https://www.airc-education.org/agency-by-country/12 ; https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.aeccglobal.com ; https://www.reddit.com/r/Indians_StudyAbroad/comments/1lzjrx9/do_you_guys_know_about_aecc_global_i_want_to_know/ ; https://www.reddit.com/r/studyAbroad/comments/1p5tli2/idp_vs_aecc_which_is_better_for_student_visa_and/

## 3. Global Reach
- Evidence: partners with all 40 universities (PIE tie with IDP); offices on QUT list in Pune, Navi Mumbai, Nagpur, Hyderabad and many more; AIRC listing shows certification period "through 2025" (could be lapsed; ask them); a search summary cites a 4.9 Google rating for Nagpur (not verified by me).
- Little independent review evidence beyond that.
- Source: https://thepienews.com/india-agents/ ; https://www.airc-education.org/agency-by-country/12

## 4. Kaaiser Services
- Evidence: positioned as an "Australian Education Specialist"; 30 Australian university partners (PIE, tied with Trans Globe); QUT-listed in Mumbai, Pune, New Delhi; Pune directory rating 8.1/10 (weak source).
- No substantive complaints or praise found beyond that.

## 5. Trans Globe
- Evidence: 30 partners (PIE); Pune office; founded 1992 per a staff profile ("40,000 students placed" claim); Pune directory 8.9/10 (weak).

## 6. VIEC
- Evidence: 27 partners (PIE); QUT-listed Pune, Mumbai x2, Hyderabad. No review evidence found.

## 7. KC Overseas Education
- Evidence: 24 partners (PIE); QUT-listed Pune, Nagpur, Hyderabad; also an agent for other Australian universities (UOW India list shows a Nagpur contact). Pune directory 8.4/10 (weak).

## Conditional / not ranked
- **Expert Education & Visa Services**: Australian-founded, offices in 23 countries; strong India Google reviews (4.7-4.9) but its Australian Trustpilot page (38 reviews, 4.0) includes serious complaints about missed visa medicals, rejections and refund difficulty (Jan 2026 and 2024 reviews). Only Hyderabad is on QUT's list near him. Pros/cons mixed.
- **Edu Forum International, Gradstar (Mumbai); UniXperts, V&U Intellect, i20fever (Hyderabad)**: on the list; I did not evaluate.
- **Smaller or aggressive-marketing agencies** warned about by Reddit users generally (large ad spend, registration fees, "guaranteed admission").

## What this ranking does NOT tell you
- Which agency gives the best outcome for QUT specifically (no data on QUT acceptance rates by agent).
- Whether any listed agency charges optional fees (e.g. IELTS coaching, visa filing, forex, loans). Ask.
- Whether commissions skew advice; none disclose rates. This is inherent to the model.
- Whether a particular branch is still operating (IDP consolidations; QUT list may lag).
