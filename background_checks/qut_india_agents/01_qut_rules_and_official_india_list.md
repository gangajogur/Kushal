# 01 - QUT Rules and Official India List

## Rule: agent required for India
- QUT "International applying" page (read 6 Nov 2026): "A QUT official representative is required for applicants who are currently outside of Australia, seeking an Australian student visa, from a nation below" - list includes **India** (also Bangladesh, Bhutan, Nepal, Pakistan, Mongolia, Myanmar, North Korea, Afghanistan, Iran, Iraq, Syria, Lebanon, Yemen, Russia, Ukraine, all of Africa, Nicaragua).
- QUT Application Portal "is configured to determine if you must apply through an approved QUT representative... If you do need to, you will not be able to continue".
- Agents lodge via QUT's **StudyLink Partner Portal**.
- Agents in Australia cannot assist these applicants unless the applicant is in Australia.
- Application fee shown on QUT page: AUD 100, non-refundable, paid in the QUT Application Portal (check how this is paid when an agent lodges; do not pay it to the agent in cash).
- Source: https://www.qut.edu.au/study/applying/international-applying ; https://cms.qut.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0004/639526/genuine-students-representative-requirements.pdf ; https://www.qut.edu.au/study/international/for-agents/advising-students

## Extra paperwork for Indian applicants (QUT agent checklist)
- Agent checklist (AC form) must accompany the application: applicant unmarried or married over a year, not intending to marry before visa lodgement; **evidence of financial capacity equal to 12 months of living costs, tuition, travel and study costs**; counselling done and a **Statement of Purpose** provided; originals sighted; QUT transfer and refund policies explained (no release before 6 months full-time); no previous visa refusal or visa application (to agent's knowledge); documents high resolution.
- Applicants from South Asia: **South Asia Genuine Student Form**.
- India-specific restricted locations (applicant "will not be considered"): Haryana (all cities except Gurgaon/Gurugram), Uttarakhand (Udham Singh Nagar, Haldwani), Rajasthan (Ganganagar, Hanumangarh), UP (Pilibhit, Lakhimpur Kheri), Himachal (Una). **Punjab**: needs special QUT approval; Punjab State Board and pathway/business restrictions apply. **Gujarat**: restrictions on pathway, business and health programs. **Maharashtra: not listed.** (Search summary of the PDF; re-read the PDF if relying on this: https://cms.qut.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/981619/qut-agent-checklist.pdf)
- Maharashtra State Board HSC applicant: QUT's published merit-scholarship rule for State Boards (90%) is covered in `report/AUSTRALIA_DEAKIN_QUT.md`.

## Official list source and method
Page: https://www.qut.edu.au/study/international/find-a-representative (footer "Page updated: 19 October 2026"). The India list is dynamic: select "India" in the country drop-down. I read it in the Cursor browser on 6 Nov 2026 via the page's rendered text. Result: **171 entries in 57 city headings** (some duplicate city spellings such as Bangalore/Bengaluru, Kochi/Cochin).
Separate section "Other agents" on the same page lists institutional partners (e.g. Alliance University, Bennett University, Presidency University, Northcap University, Karpagam Academy in India) "for information"; use the location-based list for applying.
