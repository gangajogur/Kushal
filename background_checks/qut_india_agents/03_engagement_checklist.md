# 03 - Engagement Checklist (applying to QUT through an agent)

## Before choosing
1. Ask the agency to confirm in writing that it is a **current QUT official representative for India**, which office will lodge, and the counsellor's name. Cross-check on https://www.qut.edu.au/study/international/find-a-representative (select India). Email QUT agent support (qut.agentsupport@qut.edu.au) or QUT International if unsure.
2. Check the counsellor's **QEAC number** on ICEF's QEAC list (https://www.icef.com/academy/qualified-education-agent-counsellors-qeacs-list/).
3. Ask: **"Do you receive money from QUT or anyone else for this student? Do you charge us anything, for what, and when?"** Get answers in writing. Student services are normally free; extras (IELTS coaching, visa lodgement, loans, forex, accommodation) can be paid.
4. Talk to 2-3 agencies; pick the counsellor who listens, answers quickly and does not push a fixed menu or a pathway/package you did not ask for.
5. Ask them to confirm the exact course codes: B. Information Technology (IN01, Computer Science major) and/or B. Games & Interactive Environments (IN05, Feb intake only), and that he meets the selection-rank threshold given his Maharashtra HSC.

## During the application
6. **Keep your own login/password** for any accounts (QUT StudyLink applicant account, email, IELTS, loan portals). Never give passwords. The agent submits through the StudyLink Partner Portal; ask for a copy of everything submitted and the QUT application number.
7. **Pay QUT directly** where possible (AUD 100 application fee is paid in QUT's portal per QUT's page; tuition deposits to QUT's bank details, never to the agent's personal account). No cash.
8. The **Statement of Purpose and Genuine Student Form must be his own words** (QUT requires them; agent-written templates risk refusal). Use the agent only for feedback.
9. **Financial capacity**: QUT checklist requires funds equal to 12 months of living costs, tuition, travel and study costs. Education loans are commonly used; see `../wemakescholars/05_regulation_alternatives_checklist.md`. Do not let anyone create fake or temporary funds.
10. **Visa**: in Australia, giving immigration/visa advice generally requires a registered migration agent (check OMARA register). Education counsellors may help with forms but verify who is lodging and their registration number. He can also self-lodge. (Not researched in detail here; confirm.)
11. Get QUT's Tuition Fee Refund Policy and International Student Transfer Policy in writing (QUT checklist says the agent must explain; transfer is restricted before 6 months of full-time study).
12. Apply to QUT directly through the agent but **also keep Deakin/Aizu options open** in parallel; ask whether Deakin requires an agent for India too (not checked).

## Red flags to walk away
- "Guaranteed admission/visa/scholarship".
- Asks for upfront fee just to counsel, or fees to personal accounts.
- Refuses to put terms in writing or to show the QUT agent status.
- Pushes a different university/course than the one asked.
- Suggests misstating marital status, funds, gap years or previous refusals.
- Pressure to pay a loan processing fee before written loan terms.
