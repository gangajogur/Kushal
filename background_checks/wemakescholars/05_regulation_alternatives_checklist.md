# 05 - Regulation, Alternatives, and Due-diligence Checklist

## RBI rules that apply to loan marketplaces (as of 2026)
Source: RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025 (para 6 effective 1 Nov 2025) + law-firm summaries (Vinod Kothari, KS&K, indialaw.in).
- A multi-lender **Lending Service Provider (LSP)** must show a **digital view of ALL matching loan offers**, and also name unmatched lenders.
- Each offer must show: lender name, amount, tenor, **APR**, monthly repayment, penal charges, and a link to the **Key Facts Statement (KFS)**.
- Content must be **unbiased and must not push a particular lender**, including via dark patterns. Ranking is allowed only by a **publicly pre-disclosed metric**. The matching mechanism must be documented and consistent for similar borrowers.
- All-inclusive cost (APR) must be disclosed before acceptance; **fees not in the KFS cannot be charged later**. A cooling-off period (at least one day, set by the lender) exists for digital loans.
- Not checked: whether WMS is a registered/declared LSP for each partner lender, or whether its current UI complies. Observation only: the homepage shows "starting at" rates (not APR) and masks some lenders' rates; the process complaints above (fee demanded before rate in writing) would be inconsistent with these rules if accurate.
- Where to complain: lender's grievance officer first, then RBI Integrated Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in) for bank/NBFC issues. WMS is not itself an RBI regulated entity, but its lenders are.

## Alternatives to using WMS
| Option | What it is | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Direct to SBI** (Global Ed-Vantage) | Public bank, loans up to INR 3 Cr; covers Australia, Japan, Germany etc. | Lowest fees (NIL up to INR 10 lakh; INR 5K + GST 10-20 lakh; max INR 25K + GST above), covers 80E tax benefit | Slower; branch-dependent; collateral above INR 7.5 lakh (SBI student-loan page) or above INR 50 lakh for select institutions (BankBazaar summary of Ed-Vantage) - verify current rule with the branch |
| **Other public banks** (Bank of Baroda, Central Bank of India, Canara etc.) | Direct | Low fees, no insurance push (user report) | Collateral rules, slow |
| **Vidya Lakshmi portal** (vidyalakshmi.co.in) | Government portal developed under DFS/MoE/IBA, run by Protean eGov; one form to up to 3 banks; 37-38 banks, 86-139 schemes listed | Official, no commission intermediary | Public-bank focus, less hand-holding |
| **Direct NBFC** (Credila, Avanse, Auxilo, InCred) | Apply on the lender site | Skip the intermediary; can compare same-day | Higher rates and fees; processing fee non-refundable |
| **PM-Vidyalaxmi / interest-subsidy schemes** | For domestic study mostly (Jansamarth portal for subsidy loans) | Subsidised | Generally not for overseas study; check eligibility |
| **Other intermediaries** | Eduloans (12+ lenders), Leap Finance (US PG, future-income underwriting), Credenc (domestic) | Competition for quotes | Same commission conflict; Leap/Credenc not Bachelor's-abroad fits |
Note: the Ministry of Minority Affairs' Padho Pardesh interest-subsidy scheme for study abroad was discontinued from FY2022-23 (Lok Sabha answer, 4 Feb 2026).

## Due-diligence checklist if the family still uses WMS
1. Get **each lender's offer in writing**: rate, rate type (floating/fixed), **APR**, moratorium, **PSI**, all fees, **insurance**, collateral/NACH/cheques, prepayment charges, KFS.
2. Ask WMS in writing: which lenders are included, **commission paid** by each, ranking criteria, and why a particular lender is recommended.
3. **Do not pay any processing fee** until the sanction terms are in writing and match what was promised verbally. Know the refund rule before paying.
4. Get a **direct SBI/public-bank quote in parallel**, and a direct Credila/Avanse quote for comparison.
5. Take **only the amount actually needed**; staged disbursement to the university rather than a lump sum. Do not inflate the loan for scholarship eligibility.
6. Check **tax claims (80E, TCS)** with a CA. TCS rate on overseas remittances for education depends on whether the money is loan-funded and on current thresholds (a user reported being told 5% when 2% applied; I did not verify current rates). Confirm with a CA or the bank.
7. Share ID/financial documents only on the lender's own portal where possible; ask WMS for data-sharing consent terms and withdrawal procedure; limit the number of lenders pulling credit reports (multiple hard enquiries lower CIBIL).
8. Keep copies of all chats/emails/calls; use the lender's grievance route, then RBI ombudsman if needed.
9. Verify any scholarship found on the directory at the **university/government source**.
10. Treat "government-backed" language as irrelevant to loan risk.
