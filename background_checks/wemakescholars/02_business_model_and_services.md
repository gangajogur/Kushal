# 02 - Business Model and Services

## What they do
1. **Scholarship directory** - searchable database, claims 26,000+ international scholarships "worth over $2 billion", updated weekly, "every scholarship verified" (their wording). Browsing free; account needed to check eligibility/apply.
2. **Education-loan aggregator / facilitation** - the main business today. Student fills a profile, a "Financial Officer" (FO) is assigned, documents go via a WMS portal to multiple lenders, WMS "compares and negotiates", then supports through sanction and disbursal.
3. **Scholarship test + sponsored awards tied to loans** - HEST (WMS) and Education Future International Scholarship (separate site, WMS is "processing partner").
4. **Content/support** - blog, YouTube, visa/SOP/cost-of-living guidance. Also "EliteScholars" (WMS-run fintech brand for US STEM/MBA unsecured loans, "AI/ML" assessment, up to INR 75 lakh).
5. **Referral cashback** - per T&C, loan applicants get cashback capped at INR 3,000 spread across disbursements (public bank: credited quarterly; private/NBFC: within 3 months of disbursement) and referral bonuses.

## Who pays them (revenue model)
- Students pay **no service fee** (consistent across site, T&C and third-party profiles). T&C: "WeMakeScholars doesn't collect any payment through its website... You must not pay any money to any WeMakeScholars employee."
- WMS is paid by **lenders**: referral / transaction commission **on disbursal** (akta.pro profile; WMS quote "We're paid by lenders, not by students"). Secondary: referral arrangements with education consultancies ("Preferred Partners"), details undisclosed.
- Commission rates are **not publicly disclosed**. Not found anywhere.
- Consequence: revenue is proportional to disbursed amount x lender payout rate. Incentive to (a) get a loan disbursed, (b) prefer higher-commission lenders and larger loans. This is the structural conflict of interest behind most complaints.
- Calling itself "Not-just-for-profit" (About page) sits oddly beside ~30% net margin; it is a for-profit private company.

## Loan flow as described by WMS
Check eligibility (2-min form) -> compare offers -> choose lender -> upload documents on WMS portal -> finalise paperwork -> disbursement (typically direct to university).
- Claims "typical callback within 45 mins", sanction in "10-15 days" for scholarship applicants, user-reported 7-40 days (Credila ~15 days, SBI 30-40 days in one account).
- Lenders named: SBI, Credila (HDFC Credila), ICICI, Axis, Bank of Baroda, Avanse, Auxilo, InCred, IDFC (user reports). Count claimed: 20+ (homepage), 18 (Tribune Dec 2025), 15 (DCU page), 14+ (older pages).
- Advertised rate "starting at": SBI 8.9%, Credila 10.2% (vs 12.5% "going directly"), ICICI 10.5% (vs 11.5%), Axis 10.5% (vs 11.5%). Germany page: from 8.50%. Homepage footnote: "indicative", depends on profile, lender approval, collateral. User reports of actual NBFC rates: ~11.75-12%+, "high" for no-collateral/no-strong-co-applicant cases.
- Max amount: homepage up to INR 3 Cr; collateral-free up to INR 1.5 Cr.

## Fees the student actually pays (to lenders, not WMS)
From WMS's own pages:
- Public banks (e.g. SBI): about INR 10K + GST (WMS page); SBI's own page (Sept 2026): NIL up to INR 10 lakh, INR 5K + GST for 10-20 lakh, 0.5% (max INR 25K + GST) above INR 20 lakh. Public bank fee may be refundable if rejected.
- Private banks: ~1% of sanctioned amount. NBFCs: 1-2% (up to 2% + GST). Typically paid **after sanction, before the sanction letter is issued**, and **non-refundable** even if you do not proceed.
- Other charges reported by users but not in WMS marketing: insurance (INR 60K-1 lakh deducted on a large loan), PSI (partial simple interest during moratorium), blank cheques/NACH at one NBFC.
- WMS says it negotiates fee reductions; user accounts of discounts exist.

## The HEST / Education Future link (important)
| | HEST | Education Future International Scholarship |
|---|---|---|
| Run by | WeMakeScholars | education-future.org (WMS is "processing partner") |
| Fund / size | INR 2 Cr pool; up to INR 3 lakh each | INR 2-10 lakh each |
| Who can apply | Indian citizen, >=55% in 10th/12th (EF says >60%), Bachelor/Master/MBA/Diploma, going abroad within 12 months | Similar; Bachelors, Masters |
| Loan condition | **Mandatory: loan must be processed via WMS**; self-funded students ineligible. Reason given: previous season had fake sanction letters | **Must plan to fund >=50% via a loan applied for and sanctioned via WMS**; WMS verifies the sanction |
| Odds | ~10,000+ applicants in HEST 2026; "only 2-5% get good scholarships" (WMS) | Unknown |
- A user alleges (Reddit, undated in snippet) a hidden INR 10 lakh minimum loan for Education Future and that eligibility is assessed only after paying the loan processing fee. WMS's own HEST page does not state a minimum; **unverified** but worth asking about in writing.
- Effect: the scholarship is effectively a customer-acquisition incentive for the loan business. Not independent funding.
