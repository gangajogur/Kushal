## History (company's own timeline + press)
- 2012: founders' own difficulty finding scholarships for Masters.
- Feb 2013: Facebook group "Scholarships". Grew to ~150-160K members (YourStory / LinkedIn).
- Sep 2014: says it "turned down the first investment offer"; started building the portal.
- 29 Apr 2015: portal launched by Shashi Tharoor (MP, ex-UN Under-Secretary-General).
- Sep 2015: company incorporated.
- Nov 2015 (YourStory): 8 full-time staff, no revenue model yet, "not-just-for-profit".
- 2016/2017: education-loan help begins (WMS LinkedIn says 2016; EliteScholars page says loan processing dept set up in 2017). Started with SBI, Bank of Baroda; later private banks and NBFCs.
- Jan 2016: claims NITI Aayog social-enterprise forum recognition. Oct 2017: AICTE MoU (awareness of financing at colleges).
- Jul 2025: Economic Times excellence award (per WMS). Dec 2025: Forbes India DGEMS 2025 Select 200 (Tribune press piece).
