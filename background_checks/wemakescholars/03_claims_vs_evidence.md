## Pattern of the claims
- Strong, repeated, hard-to-check authority signals (ministry, NITI Aayog, Forbes, Tharoor) used for trust.
- Hard numbers vary by page and over time.
- Consumer-facing cost framing ("zero fee", "free because Digital India") emphasises what WMS does not charge and de-emphasises lender costs and WMS's commission.
