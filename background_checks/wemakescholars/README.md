## One-paragraph summary
WeMakeScholars is a real, registered, profitable Hyderabad company (WeMakeScholars Edtech Pvt Ltd, CIN U74900TG2015PTC100548, founded 2015 by Damini Mahajan and Arjun R. Krishna). It started as a free scholarship directory (claims 26,000+ listings) and is now mainly a **study-abroad education-loan aggregator/referral channel** that is **paid commissions by lenders (banks and NBFCs), not by students**. Students pay WMS nothing directly. It is not a scam in the sense of fake or non-existent, and many users report fast sanctions and helpful "financial officers". But there is a credible, recurring pattern of complaints about NBFC steering, upfront lender processing fees, missing written interest rates, misleading tax/visa advice, and a scholarship (HEST / Education Future) that is only open to people who take a loan through WMS. Its "funded and supported by MeitY / Government of India" line is repeated everywhere but I could not find any independent or official confirmation of it.

## Bottom line (for decision use)
1. **Usable, but treat as a salesperson paid by the lender, not a neutral adviser.** Interests are not aligned with the student's: WMS earns only when a loan disburses, and (per user reports) private lenders / NBFCs and bigger loan amounts are pushed.
2. **Do not rely on it as the only source.** Get a direct quote from SBI (and/or Bank of Baroda / another public bank) and via Vidya Lakshmi, then compare against what WMS offers.
3. **Never pay a processing fee until you have the interest rate, all fees and insurance in writing (Key Facts Statement).** Private lender/NBFC fees are usually non-refundable.
4. **Ignore the "government-backed / free because of Digital India" framing** when judging risk. The loans are ordinary commercial loans with ordinary risk.
5. **Scholarship directory = discovery lead only.** Verify each award on the university/government site. Directory has shown expired entries and test/dummy entries.
6. **HEST / Education Future "scholarships" are tied to taking a loan via WMS** (up to INR 3 lakh HEST; INR 2-10 lakh Education Future; selection odds unknown). Do not take a larger or costlier loan just to qualify.
7. For a **Bachelor's** loan, a parent co-applicant is effectively mandatory at every lender (see `06_relevance_to_nephew.md`).

## Confidence
- High: company exists, founders, address/CIN, business model (lender-paid), HEST/EF loan condition (from their own terms), RBI rules.
- Medium: financials (Inc42 aggregator figures; one Tracxn table was garbled and was ignored), size (sources disagree: 58-68 vs 100-250 employees).
- Low: individual complaints and praise (anonymous, unverified, self-selected, some possibly planted on either side). Treat as a pattern signal only.
- Unverified: MeitY funding claim, "16.5% of overseas education loans", "INR 8,000 Cr+ disbursed" (other WMS-linked source said INR 4,800 Cr in Dec 2025).
