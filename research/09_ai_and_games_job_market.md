# AI's effect on junior software jobs and the games industry (2026)
Accessed 2026-10-24 via search snippets. Confidence: medium-high (academic papers + GDC survey); labelled where weak.

## Software jobs (general)
- SSRN "Is There a Junior Penalty?" (Bhatta, mid-2026): US software-dev postings fell 54% after ChatGPT release, but 58% of the fall from the 2022 peak PREDATES ChatGPT; software-developer employment kept growing; no clear wage penalty yet. => "hiring squeeze, not employment collapse".
- IZA DP 18723 (Westby, Modestino, Cheng, Jun 2026): 14-15% relative decline in JUNIOR vs senior software vacancies after ChatGPT; employers raise experience requirements; remaining junior posts shift toward problem solving, communication, attention to detail - NOT AI-specific skills.
- Brynjolfsson et al (cited by NPR, Aug 2026): early-career (22-25) workers in AI-exposed jobs (software devs) saw ~16% relative employment decline since late 2022; NY Fed: recent-grad unemployment 5.7% (Jun 2026) vs 4.1% overall; AP: CS grads face ~7.1% unemployment.
- SSRN (Biswas): decline began 2022, two years before AI-related demand surged => macro/structural causes are major; entry-level ads already demanded senior capabilities (system design). Durable skills: system design, communication, verifiable evidence of work.
- Takeaway: The degree doesn't guarantee a job; what protects juniors = demonstrable depth (portfolio, internships, systems understanding), communication, and ability to use AI tools. AI raises the bar for juniors rather than eliminating the profession.

## Games industry
- GDC 2026 State of the Industry: 28% laid off in past 2 years (33% in US); half say their employer had layoffs in last 12 months; 74% of surveyed students worry about job prospects; 87% of educators expect/see negative placement impact. 52% of game professionals think generative AI is negative for the industry (up from 30% in 2025); game programmers 59% negative. 36% use gen-AI at work (mostly LLM for research/brainstorm/code assist).
- DevQuest (Oct 2026): only 8% of ~6,100 open game roles are entry level (476); hiring is concentrated in a handful of studios. christran.gg: 542 entry-level game jobs, engineering only 82; top hirers Keywords Studios, Side (outsourcing/QA), Sportradar, bet365 (iGaming) - i.e. not AAA studios.
- AUTOMATON (Japan): recruiters cite background/prop artists and ASSISTANT PROGRAMMERS (and testing/debug) as jobs most impacted by generative AI in Japan; studios seek people who can integrate/direct AI in pipelines.
- Takeaway: Games is the highest-risk target career: crowded, layoff-prone, low entry-level share. Game-AI/gameplay-systems skills are transferable to robotics, simulation, ML engineering, real-time systems, and AI agent products -> must keep non-games exits open. This is a strong argument for a broad CS/AI degree (not a game-specific diploma) plus a portfolio.
