# ISSE (English-medium) course list by semester
Source: Japanese curriculum page https://www.ritsumei.ac.jp/ise/education/curriculum/ (last row = ISSE English course), plus https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/ise/education/feature/ and https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/ise/isse/about/
Accessed: 2026-10-24 via web search. Confidence: high (official), but "course names may change". Row layout is inferred from table; semester placement is approximate where the table was flattened.

## Facts
- Degree: Bachelor of Engineering, 4 years, ALL instruction in English. Campus: Osaka Ibaraki Campus (OIC) (moved from Biwako-Kusatsu in Apr 2024). ISSE is 1 of 7 courses in College of Information Science and Engineering (other 6 are Japanese-medium).
- "Units" (cross-course AI / Data Science / DX / IoT bundles) are introduced to ISSE from AY2028 (so not available to a 2027 entrant until later, check).
- Graduation research: from semester 6 (autumn of year 3), lab chosen in spring of year 3.

## Common core (all courses incl. ISSE share a base)
Maths: Maths 1-4, Engineering Mathematics 1-4, Info Basic Maths, Probability & Statistics, Fourier analysis, Differential Equations, Multivariate analysis, Discrete Maths, Numerical Analysis, Statistical Analysis/Simulation/Modeling, Optimization and Control Theory.
Info: Info Theory, Info Ethics, Intro to Computer Science, Intro to Info Systems Engineering, Professional Ethics, Logic circuits, Experimental Design, Software Engineering, Computer Networks, Digital Signal Processing, Computer Organization (Architecture), Database, Operating Systems, Network Security, Computer Graphics, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.
Global IT: Information Science in Action, Presentation Plus 401, Writing for Publication 402.
Graduation research: Grad Research 1-3.

## ISSE-specific (English) sequence (as it appears)
- PBL1: Problem Analysis and Modeling; Intro to Programming; Intro to OOA, OOD and UML  (Sem 1)
- PBL2: Team-based Design; Programming Practice 1; Programming Language (Sem 2)
- PBL3: Creative Design; Programming Practice 2; Data Structures and Algorithms (Sem 3)
- PBL4: Team-based Creative Design; Imperative Programming + Practice; Network Systems; Human Interface (Sem 4)
- PBL5: Design Evolution; Distributed Systems; Web Information Engineering; Image Processing; Systems Ergonomics (Sem 5)
- Sem 6+: Introduction to Robotic Systems; Data Science; Data Visualization; Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning
- Career: "Information and Professions", Overseas IT English / IT training programs, Global Internship, IT certification practice.
