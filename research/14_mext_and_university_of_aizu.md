## University of Aizu (UoA), Aizu-Wakamatsu, Fukushima - public university dedicated to computer science
- ICT Global All-English Program: 4-year BSc in Computer Science and Engineering, ALL instruction in English; Japanese NOT required at admission; Japanese classes + Aizu culture classes provided. Admission = document screening (online interview if needed). Faculty ~40% international (16 countries); student:faculty ~10:1; average class <=20; curriculum follows ACM/IEEE CS2013; internships in Japan/overseas; project/research opportunities; buddy system.
