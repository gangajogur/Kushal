Rubric = same 0-3 skill scale as 31_curriculum_matrix_v2 (max 28.5). Scores here are ESTIMATES from structure/elective lists, not full syllabus reads. Confidence per college below.

## WIT Solapur (local, unaided autonomous, CSE 180 seats) - confidence LOW-MEDIUM
Seen: official page https://witsolapur.org/syllabus-cse/ (S.Y./T.Y./Final syllabi as PDFs - not read); Honours in AI & ML (extra credits on top of the B.Tech): Foundations & Applications of ML (sem IV), REINFORCEMENT LEARNING (sem V: DP, Monte Carlo, TD learning), NLP (sem VI), Deep Learning + mini project (sem VII) (https://witsolapur.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/TY-B-Tech-Hons-AI-and-ML-syllabus.pdf); also Honours in Data Science. Exam scheme ESE 60 + ISE 40 + ICA 25 (exam-weighted). The Data Structures syllabus (seen for the Electronics & Computer branch, same institute) teaches memory allocation in C++, trees/graphs/heaps/hashing/tries.
NOT confirmed: WIT's core CSE list (OS, architecture, AI, graphics etc.) - the aggregator listing (universitykart) is generic/unreliable; a "Walchand" SY/TY list found is WCE SANGLI, not WIT Solapur. No evidence of a graphics, game, or C++ systems course.
Estimated score: ~10 base (35%), ~11-12 with AI&ML Honours (40%).

## COEP Technological University, Pune (CSE) - confidence MEDIUM-HIGH (official NEP structure)
Sem V: Design & Analysis of Algorithms, Computer Networks, OPERATING SYSTEMS (each 4 cr with lab), DE-1, internship (3 cr), minors; Sem VI: DBMS, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (+lab), Crypto & Network Security, DE-2. Dept electives by track: System Software/HW (POSIX programming, Parallel Computer Architecture & Programming, GPU computing, Multicore, Embedded, Distributed systems, Storage virtualisation); AI (Machine Learning, Data Visualisation, NLP, Deep Learning, GENERATIVE AI); Algorithms/Programming (Advanced Data Structures, Functional Programming, OO Modeling & Design, Parallel Algorithms, GRAPHICS AND MULTIMEDIA, Design Patterns); older revision also has Computer Graphics (+lab), Computational Geometry, Digital Signal & Image Processing, and credit language courses incl. JAPANESE and GERMAN. C++ not a separate course (procedural + OOP). Assessment: MSE 30 / TA 10 / ESE 60 (exam-weighted). Honours/Minors available; AI&ML branch exists (AIML SY 2026-27).
Estimated score ~16-17 (58%): DS&A 3, Systems 2, Maths 2, Graphics 1, AI 3, Game 0, Team 2 (internship + mini projects), Research 1.

## VNIT Nagpur CSE (NIT) - confidence MEDIUM-LOW
Electives include Computer Graphics, Image Processing, AI, ML, Deep Learning, Game Theory, Fuzzy; core: OS, compilers, networks, computer organisation, algorithms (https://vnit.ac.in/engineering/cse/academics/). Est. ~16-18 (60%).

## IIIT Pune CSE - confidence MEDIUM
Core: algorithms, computer organisation, OS, DBMS, networks, theory of computation, software eng, AI; electives: deep learning, NLP, computer vision, distributed systems, crypto, formal methods, quantum; year-long final project. No graphics/game/RL confirmed. Est. ~15-17.

## Takeaways
- Indian CSE/AI-ML curricula = solid CS core (DS&A, OS, networks, DBMS) + good AI/ML electives (AI, ML, NLP, DL, sometimes RL/GenAI) at tier-1/2; weak on graphics, game, real-time/low-level C++ and systems depth; assessment is exam-weighted (ESE 50-60%) rather than project-based; mini-projects and internships exist but are small. No Indian curriculum found with a game-AI lab/PBL like ISSE's ICE lab.
- Best of the researched Indian options ~58-65% vs ISSE 81%, Aizu/h_da 75%, Abertay 88%. Local WIT ~35-40% base (AI/ML honours helps).
- Gaps are fillable by self-study (C++, graphics, engines, RL) - but must be done in addition to a heavier exam-driven workload; the 2-year Master's abroad would then provide the research/lab part.
- Language: COEP offered Japanese/German credit courses (2019-23 revision; verify still offered).
