# AIE (Academy of Interactive Entertainment) - non-university games programming, Australia
Sources: https://aie.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/International-Course-Fees-Feb-2026-v1.2.pdf ; https://aie.edu.au/course/game-programming/ ; https://aieinstitute.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/fee-schedule-international-students-2026.pdf
- Advanced Diploma of Professional Game Development (Game Programming): 2 years full time; international fee AUD 65,000 total (Y1 two diplomas 16,250 each; Y2 32,500), Feb 2026 intake. Teaches C++, C#, OpenGL, game engines; 3 cross-discipline game projects (with artists/designers). Campuses Sydney, Melbourne, Canberra, Adelaide.
- AIE Institute Bachelor of Game Development (Game Programming major), international: 2027 AUD 21,608/semester, 6 semesters (~AUD 130k full course at 2026 price; articulation from Adv Dip gives 120 CP credit => ~AUD 65k + ~AUD 65k).
- VET Student Loans/FEE-HELP are for domestic students only. Visa: 500 student; post-study 485 only if the qualification meets the CRICOS/AQF higher-ed criteria (Advanced Diploma likely does NOT qualify for the 2-yr 485 higher-ed stream; verify) - a risk.
- Pros: portfolio-first, C++, directly games, shorter. Cons: not a university degree (diploma AQF 6), expensive per year (AUD 32.5k/yr), narrow, weaker brand, poor for non-games Plan B, visa/work rights weaker.
