## Admission requirements (ISSE, April intake, AO English-basis)
- Documents: academic transcripts, application essay, English test score, letter(s) of reference; interview "if required". No SAT-based pathway for ISSE; SAT-EBRW only accepted by JDP (580) as an English proof - NOT ISSE.
- English minimums (round dependent): TOEFL iBT 72-76 (or 4.0 on new 1-6 scale), IELTS 5.5-6.0, TOEIC L&R 730, Duolingo English Test 105-110. Nephew's DET 130 -> comfortably meets (verify DET validity <2 yrs at time of application).
- Recommended prep (not a hard requirement): Japanese-curriculum-level maths I/II/III/A/B/C equivalent. Maths 82%, SAT-M 690 should be OK but the course is maths-heavy.
- Must satisfy MEXT's university-entrance qualification (12 years of schooling) - Indian HSC with 12 years qualifies; verify specifics for state boards (Maharashtra HSC generally accepted; check "12 years" rule, he completed 10+2 = 12 years).
- Selection is competitive for the tuition-reduction categories based on the admission result.

## Scholarships (Ritsumeikan Tuition Reduction Scheme)
- Category I: 100% reduction (top ~10% of admission capacity), Category II: 50% (next ~10%), Category III: 20% (all other admitted international students w/ "Student" status). Year 1 decided by admission result; later years by grades.
- !!! Scheme "scheduled to change from AY2027" and for 2028 onward "currently undecided; may be partially discontinued or revamped." => cannot bank on it. Use 0% and 20% as scenarios in cost model.
- Other: JASSO (monthly stipend ~48,000 JPY for private-expense international students, but selective and for those already enrolled - verify), MEXT govt scholarship (very competitive).

## Career outcomes (College of Information Science and Engineering, all courses; not ISSE only)
- Current page: 50.4% go to graduate school, 49.6% employed/other. Older ISSE page: 45.6% grad school / 54.4% employed. Pamphlet: "Nearly 40%" of English-medium program graduates go to grad school.
- Industry: IT 47.7%, Manufacturing 22.3%, Service 15.2%, Telecom/Media 4.6%.
- Undergrad employed in Research/Dev/Tech roles: 28.9%; Grad-school grads: 47.2%. Big/large companies: undergrad 30.5% vs Master 39.9%.
- Univ-wide employment rate 96.8% (AY2024).
- Employers of English-medium graduates (all programs): Accenture Japan, Amazon Japan, Canon, Dentsu, JAL, Microsoft Japan, MUFG, NTT Docomo Business, Rakuten, SoftBank, P&G Japan, etc. Grad schools: Stanford, Columbia, UW, Chicago, UBC, Oxford, LSE, Edinburgh, NUS, Tokyo, Kyoto.
