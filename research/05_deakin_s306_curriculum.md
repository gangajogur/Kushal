# Deakin Bachelor of Computer Science (S306, v2, 2027)
Source: https://handbook.deakin.edu.au/current-students-courses/course.php?course=S306&version=2&year=2027&keywords=Computer+Science (fetched OK, 2026-10-24). Confidence: high. Fees NOT on this page (see 07_australia_costs_visa.md).
Campus: Burwood (Melbourne) or Online. 3 years full time, 24 credit points (8 units/yr, 4 per trimester, 2 trimesters/yr; optional T3 to fast-track). ACS accredited (Seoul Accord). AQF Level 7. Articulates into Bachelor of IT (Honours) S470 (4th year).
Compulsory work placement (capstone options: SIT344 Professional Practice, SIT378 Team Project B, or SIT306 IT Placements and Industry Experience). Equipment ~AUD 500.

## Core units (13 + 3 capstone)
Y1 T1: SIT102 Intro to Programming; SIT111 Computer Systems; SIT192 Discrete Maths; SIT112 Intro to Data Science and AI
Y1 T2: SIT232 Object-Oriented Development; SIT103 Database Fundamentals; SIT292 Linear Algebra for Data Analysis; SIT202 Computer Networks & Communication
Y2 T1: SIT221 Data Structures and Algorithms; SIT215 Computational Intelligence; + 2 electives
Y2 T2: SIT223 Professional Practice in IT; SIT320 Advanced Algorithms; SIT315 Concurrent and Distributed Programming; + 1 elective
Y3 T1: SIT374 Team Project A (Project Mgmt) + 3 electives
Y3 T2: capstone (2 cp) + 2 electives
Majors: Computational Mathematics, Data Science (Stats, Data Wrangling, Machine Learning SIT307, Deep Learning SIT319, Software Architecture IoT, NLP SIT330), Internet of Things, Robotics (Robotics Studio, Embedded, Robotics App Dev, Concurrent, Computer Vision & Speech).
Minors: Comp Maths, Cloud, Cyber Security, Embedded Systems, Full Stack Development.

## Fit vs game-AI/gameplay-systems goals
- STRONG on: AI/ML (Intro DS&AI, Computational Intelligence, ML, Deep Learning, NLP with Data Science major), algorithms (DSA + Advanced Algorithms), concurrency (SIT315), linear algebra, discrete maths, work placement + team project capstones.
- WEAK/ABSENT: no computer graphics, no game programming/engine/physics unit, no OS unit named (Computer Systems is intro), no explicit C/C++ systems programming, no reinforcement learning/game AI, no thesis. Mostly Python/Java/C# style (UNVERIFIED - check SIT102 language).
- Intelligent & robotics orientation: good for "applied domain-specific AI + hardware/software integration" (his secondary interest, Q3) via Robotics/IoT majors.
- 3 years only (vs 4) -> less tuition, earlier earning.
- Extra-curricular: competitions, Deakin Incubator.
