## Deakin

**Bachelor of Computer Science, S306 (Burwood, Melbourne).** 24 credit points: 13 core units, 3 capstone credit points, 8 flexible units. ACS-accredited. Compulsory work-integrated learning; capstone options include placements. Equipment about AUD 500.
- Core includes Computational Intelligence (SIT215), Concurrent and Distributed Programming (SIT315), Advanced Algorithms (SIT320), Discrete Mathematics, Linear Algebra for Data Analysis, Introduction to Data Science and AI.
- Majors: Computational Mathematics, Data Science, Internet-of-Things, **Robotics** (units SIT122, SIT210, SIT225, SIT310, SIT315, SIT332).
- Minors: Embedded Systems (adds SIT329), Game Design (SIT151, SIT253, SIT254, SIT283; design- and VR-leaning), VR/AR, Cloud, Cyber, Full Stack.

**Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence, S308.** Same fee (AUD 43,800 for 2027), 17 core units. Adds Machine Learning (SIT307), Deep Learning (SIT319), Natural Language Processing (SIT330), Robotics/Computer Vision/Speech (SIT332), Data Wrangling.

**Reinforcement Learning (SIT796)** is a postgraduate unit. Prerequisites list exceptions for S464 and master's codes only, so I found no undergraduate pathway. The Honours coursemap (S406) includes 4 credit points at level 7 [UNVERIFIED whether this reaches SIT796].

## QUT

**Bachelor of Information Technology, 418801.** 288 credit points: core 108, major 72, QUT You 24, complementary 84 (a second 72-credit major plus an elective, or a minor plus electives). ACS-accredited. Final-year capstone with a real client (217 students and 48 partners in 2024).
- Majors: Artificial Intelligence, Business Analysis, Computer Science, Cyber Security, Enterprise Computing, Process Analytics, Software Development.
- **Computer Science major** includes High Performance and Parallel Computing (CAB401), Systems Programming (CAB403), Machine Learning (IFN580), Cloud Computing (CAB432), Blockchain, Algorithms and Complexity.
- **AI major** includes ML for Decision Making (CAB330), Machine Learning, Generative AI (IFB320), Statistical ML and Data Analytics.
- Embedded Systems (EGH456) is an engineering unit and needs CAB403.
- Whether CS plus AI can be taken as two majors was **not confirmed**.

**Games and Interactive Environments (Software Technologies), 418102 / IN05.** 3 years, February intake only, selection rank 70.
- Game Engine Theory and Application (IGB283: graphics maths, 3D engines), Game Studio 1 and 2 (team projects), capstone to publish a game.
- Creative-leaning core: Interactive Media for Games (IGB101), IGB120, IGB180, Level Design (IGB321).
- Marketing mentions options in graphics programming and game AI; **I could not confirm a named game-AI unit.**
- Minors include Mathematics, Advanced Software Technologies, Software Technologies.
- 2027 fee **unverified** (older figure AUD 44,300). Accreditation **unverified**.

## UWA

**Bachelor of Science (BP004), Computer Science major (MJD-CMPSC), Perth, 3 years, ACS-accredited.** Handbook: https://www.handbooks.uwa.edu.au/majordetails?code=MJD-CMPSC
- Level 3 core: Advanced Algorithms (CITS3001), Computer Networks (CITS3002), Professional Computing (CITS3200), Agile Web Development (CITS3403).
- Level 3 options (take 6 points): Graphics and Animation (CITS3003; OpenGL, GLSL, 3D animation), Knowledge Representation (CITS3005), Secure Coding (CITS3007), CS WIL Internship (CITS3009), Intelligent Agents (CITS3011), Human-Computer Interaction (CITS3203), High Performance Computing (CITS3402).
- **No machine learning or deep learning in the BSc.** Machine Learning (CITS5508), Deep Learning (CITS5017), NLP (CITS4012), Computer Vision (CITS4402) are Level 4–5 and open to Honours or master's students.
- **AI major (MJD-ARIDM) exists only in the Bachelor of Advanced Computer Science (Honours), BH008** (4 years, AUD 54,400/yr, ATAR 90 or equivalent, proposed 92, WAM 70 to reach Honours; Maths Methods scaled 50). Transfer rule: at least 24 points done, WAM 70, Maths Methods or MATH1721 plus 12 points from CITS1401/1402/1003. The admission rule says ATAR 90, assured pathway or access program, so whether BSc students can transfer needs written confirmation.
- Embedded Systems (ELEC3020) is an engineering core unit, offered Semester 2 only, not a CS option.
- No games degree. Graphics and Animation (Semester 1 only) is the nearest unit.

## APU

**BSc (Hons) Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence), 3 years, Kuala Lumpur.** https://www.apu.edu.my/course/bsc-hons-in-computer-science-ai
- Year 1: Introduction to Networking, Systems Software and Computing Concepts, Introduction to Databases, Digital Thinking and Innovation, Python Programming, Systems Analysis and Design, Integrated Computer Systems, Fundamentals of Entrepreneurship, Mathematical Concepts for Computing, Introduction to AI, Introduction to Object Oriented Programming.
- Year 2: Innovation Process, Research Methods, System Development Methods, OO Development with Java, Systems and Network Administration, Web Applications, Concurrent Programming, Computer Systems Low Level Technique, Data Structures, Programming for Data Analysis, **AI Methods**, Imaging and Special Effects. **16-week internship.**
- Year 3: Venture Building, Project Management, Investigations in AI, Algorithmics, User Experience, Advanced Database Systems, Text Analytics and Sentiment Analysis, **Image Processing, Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition**, **Further AI**, **Optimization and Deep Learning**, Project in AI.
- Compulsory Malaysian Qualifications Agency subjects (ethics, Malay communication, philosophy, workplace skills, integrity, co-curriculum).
- APU states an Nvidia DGX Spark AI lab. Actual depth of units not checked.
- Separate APU degrees exist for Computer Games Development, VR/AR, Interactive Media. Not researched.

## Verdict (judgement)

- **QUT Games degree:** best fit for engine and gameplay programming.
- **Deakin BAI:** deepest AI at bachelor's level.
- **Deakin BCS (Robotics or Embedded):** best for hardware plus AI.
- **QUT BIT, Computer Science major (possibly with an AI second major):** best overall balance, and in Brisbane.
- **UWA BSc:** theory-solid but misses his stated interests unless he reaches the ATAR 90 degree.
- **APU:** most AI content for the price, but a weaker brand and weak work-abroad route (see report 10.7).
