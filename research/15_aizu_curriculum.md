## What is confirmed
- Curriculum follows ACM/IEEE CS Curricula (CS2013); 4-year BSc Computer Science and Engineering; 4-quarter system; small-group active learning; research lab + supervised graduation thesis (labs joined in senior years); internships in Japan or third countries encouraged.
- Course codes seen: PL04 C++ Programming (!); IT01 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (3rd yr, 4 credits; follows AIMA by Russell & Norvig: state/feature representation, uninformed/informed search BFS/DFS/UCS/A*, adversarial search minimax, plus language modeling topics; prerequisites linear algebra I-II, calculus I-II, probability & statistics, automata & languages); IT02 COMPUTER GRAPHICS (3rd yr, 3 credits: OpenGL, 3D viewing pipeline, lighting/shading, texture mapping, skeleton and PHYSICS-BASED ANIMATION, ray tracing, rasterization, geometric modelling, GPU programming GLSL vertex/fragment shaders); IT03 Image Processing; IT05 Robotics and Automatic Control; IT06 HUMAN INTERFACE AND VIRTUAL REALITY (Unity, Blender, game-engine programming, VR/AR/MR, group projects - per search synthesis; verify); IT08 Signal Processing and Linear Systems; CN03 Network Programming; CN04 Wireless Networking; N03/N04 network design / digital comms; FU08 Automata and Languages; MA01-MA07 Linear Algebra I-II, Calculus I-II, Probability & Statistics.
- Japanese language: credit courses at Basic/Intermediate/Advanced/Business levels + non-credit JLPT prep (credit-bearing, unlike Ritsumeikan ISSE). "Culture and History of Aizu" in English. Employment Support Office (Japanese job-hunting coaching); official FAQ: for job hunting "generally at least N2 of JLPT" is required. "Many international students who graduated from UoA are working in the best Japanese companies" (claim).
- Admission: documents only (online interview if needed); accepts SAT/IB/ACT/A-levels; no Japanese needed at admission.
