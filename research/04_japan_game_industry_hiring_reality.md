## Verdict on nephew's claim "Nintendo/Capcom actively recruit freshers from the university"
PARTLY TRUE, with big caveats:
1. TRUE: Japanese studios run annual shinsotsu (new-grad) hiring. Both Nintendo and Capcom explicitly accept applicants regardless of major, and welcome international students. Capcom's intl message: "many international employees".
2. CAVEAT 1 - LANGUAGE: Dev work is in Japanese. Game Freak: "business-level Japanese (N1)". 1-UP Studio: JLPT N1-equivalent guideline. AMATA: N2. Bandai Namco Pictures: N1 required by start. Capcom info sessions and selection are in Japanese ("English support at selection: speak in Japanese"). Nintendo: no stated JLPT but the same practical reality (inference). => He MUST reach ~N2 minimum, realistically N1, by the time he applies (job hunting happens in year 3 - mid-year 4, i.e. ~2 years before graduation!).
3. CAVEAT 2 - TIMING: Capcom's cycle: entry opens ~Dec of year before, closes ~Aug (2027 cycle closed 10 Aug 2026 for grads up to Mar 2027). Nintendo 2028 cycle accepts grads Apr 2025-Mar 2028, join 1 Apr 2028. So job hunting happens in the 3rd year, i.e. nephew (enter Apr 2028, grad Mar 2032) would apply ~2030-31. His thesis lab starts autumn of year 3 - he must hunt AND start thesis at the same time.
4. CAVEAT 3 - "Ritsumeikan recruits": Companies hire by individual application, not via a university pipeline. Ritsumeikan has a career office (no guarantee). Old lab data shows SEGA hired 3 from one lab, i.e. small numbers.
5. CAVEAT 4 - SCALE: Nintendo hires only ~119 new grads/year across ALL roles (2026: 86 technical incl. design/sound/planner + 29 office + 4 vocational). Highly competitive globally; the number of foreign hires is tiny. Capcom similar order of magnitude.
6. NOT NEEDED: Master's. Capcom pays a FLAT base >= 300,000 JPY/month "for graduate degree holders to vocational degree holders" (since FY2025); Game Freak says school type/major has NO effect on selection; AMATA flat 240k. Nintendo pays small premium: Bachelor 306,300 / Master 317,100 / PhD 334,300 JPY/month (Apr 2026). So the nephew is right that a Master's isn't a hard requirement in games (differs from manufacturing/R&D where Master's matters). But portfolio + coding test matter most (Game Freak: "portfolio and practical exam", Capcom: portfolio for some roles).
7. C++ expected: Game Freak uses C++ for game implementation, C# for tools; no C++ background OK if strong in another language, learn by join date. Nintendo/Capcom use C++ (Capcom RE Engine) (general knowledge). ISSE's course list shows no named C++ course -> self-study needed.

- AMATA 240,000 flat incl. 45h overtime.
- Rough annual (Capcom): ~4.5-5M JPY incl. bonus (ESTIMATE). Compare: Japan average game-dev pay is moderate vs Western studios/big tech; overseas big-tech/Japanese tech (Rakuten, Mercari, etc) pays more.
- Visa: for a bachelor's graduate with a tech job: "Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services" work visa; employer sponsors (VERIFY).

## Implications
- Japan path is viable ONLY if nephew commits to Japanese (N2 by early year 3, ideally N1). He said he's willing. The ISSE English-only course does not teach Japanese by itself; Ritsumeikan offers Japanese language classes (verify credits/intensity). 
- Alternative: use Japan degree to get non-games tech jobs (IT 47.7% of grads) - e.g. foreign-friendly employers (Rakuten, Mercari, LINE-Yahoo, Sony, Rakuten, Accenture) where English is OK. This is the hedge.
- Alternative: the Japan degree + English-speaking game studios abroad. The degree is internationally recognised (Ritsumeikan is a mid-top private Japanese univ; not in global top 200 for CS).
