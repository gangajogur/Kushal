# Cost model (all-in, whole degree). Planning estimates, NOT quotes.
FX (RBI 23-24 Oct 2026): 100 JPY = Rs 60.49 | 1 EUR = Rs 106.45 | 1 AUD = Rs 66.67. Rs 1 crore = 100 lakh.
Assumes fees held at published 2026/27 levels (+ modest indexation for AU). Living costs use mid-point assumptions. Excludes loan interest. Part-time income NOT netted off.

Time-to-degree: Studienkolleg route ~4.5 yrs; India-year route ~4.5 yrs total; both longer than Deakin/UWA (3 yrs).

## Payback sanity check (very rough; salary figures are estimates - flagged)
- Japan game programmer: Capcom base JPY 300k/mo x 12 + bonuses ~ JPY 4.5-5M (~Rs 27-30 lakh gross); net after tax/insurance ~80% => ~Rs 22 lakh; minus living ~Rs 10-12 lakh => ~Rs 10-12 lakh/yr saved. A Rs 80-90 lakh bill = ~7-9 years of savings; a loan at ~9-11% interest makes it worse. NOT a good financial ROI if loan-funded and games-only.
- Germany junior software/AI dev: EUR 46-55k gross (Blue Card floor EUR 45.9k) = Rs 49-59 lakh gross; net ~Rs 33-38 lakh; savings maybe Rs 12-18 lakh/yr. Rs 60-72 lakh cost => ~4-5 yrs. Much better ROI.
- Australia junior software dev: ~AUD 70-85k gross (~Rs 47-57 lakh) [general knowledge, unverified]; cost Rs 140-170 lakh => ~8-10 yrs: worst ROI of the three.
- India: Rs 4-12 lakh typical fresher (higher at top firms); cost Rs 10-30 lakh => 2-4 yrs.
