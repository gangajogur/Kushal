# Deggendorf Institute of Technology (DIT) B.Sc. Artificial Intelligence (English) - module list
Sources: https://www.th-deg.de/ain-b-en ; exam regulations (2025) https://ec.dit.edu/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Pruefungsordnungen/Bachelor/artificial_intelligence_bachelor_2025_2.pdf ; module guide WS25/26 https://th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Modulhandbuecher/modulhandbuch-ain-b-ws25-26.pdf (129 KB cached). Accessed 2026-10-24. Confidence: high (official list).

## Facts
- B.Sc., 7 semesters, 210 ECTS, English-taught (B2 English needed at application; German A2 required by end of studies), practical semester 5 (30 ECTS internship), thesis 12 ECTS + seminar. Fees: see 21 (EUR 500/semester non-EU in Bavaria per earlier note - re-verify before applying).
- Sem1: Mathematics 1, Programming 1, Foundations of CS, Operating Systems and Networks, Introduction to AI, Key Competencies 1.
- Sem2: Mathematics 2, Programming 2, ALGORITHMS AND DATA STRUCTURES, Internet Technologies, Computational Logic, Key Comp 2.
- Sem3: Databases, Statistics, Project Management, Assistance Systems, AI PROGRAMMING, Key Comp 3 or German.
- Sem4: NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING, Human Factors & HMI, MACHINE LEARNING, COMPUTER VISION, Software Engineering, Key Comp 4 or German.
- Sem5: internship (30 ECTS).
- Sem6: Seminar Current Topics in AI, AUTONOMOUS ROBOTICS, AI Project, DEEP LEARNING/BIG DATA, elective 1, Key Comp 5/German.
- Sem7: electives 2-4 (incl. AI Applications 1+2), Bachelor Seminar, Bachelor Thesis.

## Fit
- STRONG: maths (2 modules + statistics + logic), OS, DS&A, ML, NLP, CV, DL, robotics, internship, thesis - the best AI/ML depth of any option (more than ISSE or Aizu by module count). No graphics module, no C++ or game engine content, no game-specific content (gap -> self-learning/projects). Systems-level: OS+networks module only; no computer architecture explicit.
- Admission: no aptitude test (typical Bavarian HAW) - route = Studienkolleg T-Kurs (it matches: maths/CS). Plus 6 theory semesters, 18-month job-seeker visa, Bavarian cost low. Nephew who wants "AI for games" would need to build the game side himself (game jams, Unity/Unreal, C++).
