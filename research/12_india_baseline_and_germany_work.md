## India (cost baseline; 4-year B.Tech CSE; Maharashtra HSC eligible: needs PCM; entrance via JEE Main / MHT-CET)
- NIT CSE/CSE-AIML: Rs 7.75 lakh total over 4 yrs (NIT Durgapur-type fee sheet); IIT/NIT ~ Rs 6-9 lakh + hostel Rs 40-60k/yr => ~Rs 8-11 lakh all-in. IIIT (PPP) CSE Rs 4-12 lakh (careers360 says some IIITs Rs 18-22.5 lakh).
- State govt colleges (COEP, VJTI, PICT Pune): ~Rs 1.2-2 lakh/yr (high cutoffs: 99+ percentile MHT-CET).
- Private: MIT-WPU CSE Rs 4.1 lakh/yr; JSPM Univ Rs 2.05 lakh/yr (CSE-AIML); VIT Rs 1.95-4.9 lakh/yr; SRM Rs 4.75 lakh/yr; Manipal MIT Rs 6.08 lakh/yr; Thapar Rs 6.5 lakh/yr.
- => Private B.Tech all-in ~Rs 12-30 lakh (tuition + hostel) vs Rs 70-170 lakh abroad.
- Unknown: nephew's JEE / MHT-CET results (he did SAT instead). ASK USER. Entry to NIT/IIT needs JEE; private colleges accept 50% HSC + CET/JEE or management quota.
- Game-dev/AI: Indian degrees are generic CS (AI/ML branches common); game industry in India is small (mobile) but remote work for global studios is possible; Indian CS-AI curriculum quality varies greatly by college (tier-1 great, tier-3 weak).
- Link to Germany: one successful year of an Indian bachelor's + 70% HSC + APS = direct entry to German subject-restricted programmes (skip Studienkolleg).

## Germany: after graduation (official)
- 18-month residence permit after German degree to find a job; may work meanwhile (BAMF).
- EU Blue Card 2026: standard threshold EUR 50,700 gross; reduced EUR 45,934.20 for IT specialists and for graduates within 3 years of degree (Arbeitsagentur/Make it in Germany). So a junior job paying >= ~EUR 46k gives a Blue Card -> PR path (21-27 months w/ German B1 - general knowledge, verify).
- German games industry (game.de, Mar 2026): 956 companies (474 dev-only studios, 427 dev+publish, 55 publishers); 12,235 employees at developers/publishers (-3% YoY); >30,000 jobs total. Small vs Japan/US but exists; few large studios (Ubisoft Blue Byte, Crytek, Yager, InnoGames, Wooga etc. - general knowledge).
