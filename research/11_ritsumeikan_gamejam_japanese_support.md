# Ritsumeikan: Game Jam claim + Japanese language support
Sources: https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/news/detail/?id=307 ; https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/news/detail/?id=309 ; https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/ise/event/article.html/?id=2 ; https://doi.org/10.1145/3316287.3316291 ; ISSE FAQ https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/ise/isse/faq/ ; Japanese placement guidebook https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/file.jsp?id=581245 ; https://www.ritsumei.ac.jp/nihongo/education/
Accessed 2026-10-24. Confidence: high for 2018 events; unknown whether recurring "twice a year".

## Game Jam claim
- VERIFIED but only partly: Ritsumeikan CISE (ISSE English students + Japanese-track students + Kyoto Computer Gakuin) held an international Game Jam on 25-26 May 2018 with Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT, School of Interactive Games and Media, USA), 10 RIT students, 8 teams, 10-hour jam, Unity used; documented in a 2019 ACM paper. Participants from ISSE said they got Unity and teamwork experience.
- NOT verified: that it "happens twice a year" or is still running in 2026. (Possibly a nephew's/uni's recruiting claim.) ASK the uni.
- Also: RIT is a very strong games school; collaboration exists at faculty level.

## Japanese language
- ISSE regular curriculum has NO Japanese classes. CISE offers FREE extracurricular Japanese classes for ISSE students, NON-credit (FAQ). Credits earned at other universities cannot be transferred into ISSE.
- Univ-wide Japanese program (levels Beginner -> aiming N1) is mainly for English-basis students in other colleges (GLA etc.); JLPT N3/N2/N1 prep courses are non-credit (GLA-run).
- A search result mentioned a 2-year IT-talent Japanese + business Japanese + job-prep program starting ~N2 level (source not confirmed - verify with Ritsumeikan career office).
- Therefore reaching N2 within ~2 years of arrival will be mostly nephew-driven (study 10-15 h/week + immersion), while carrying a heavy maths/engineering load. He should start Japanese BEFORE arrival (gap year = good: N5->N3 doable).
- Living in Japan 4 yrs + part-time jobs (Japanese speaking) helps.
