## Visa and living
- Student visa (subclass 500) fee AUD 2,500 from 1 Jul 2026 (official: studyaustralia.gov.au). Financial capacity: ~AUD 29,710 living for 12 months + tuition + travel (yocket; official figures change - verify). Genuine Student requirement. OSHC health cover ~AUD 500-700/yr (nominal; verify).
- Realistic living: Melbourne/Perth AUD ~25-35k/yr (own estimate; verify).
- Work rights: students may work limited hours/fortnight (currently 48 h/fortnight - VERIFY; changed 2023-24).
- Post-study: Temporary Graduate visa (485) Post-Higher Education Work: 2 years for a bachelor's (Indian nationals AI-ECTA: 3 years only for first-class honours in STEM/ICT = needs a 4th (honours) year); fee AUD 5,750 (Jul 2026); age <= 35. After that PR needs skilled migration points - harder/tighter in 2025-26 (not researched; low confidence).

## Games relevance (light)
- Neither Deakin nor UWA has game dev/graphics/engine focus in the core. UWA: Graphics & Animation + Intelligent Agents as ONE option (level 3) + Systems Programming in C. Deakin: ML/DL/NLP, Robotics, no graphics.
- Australian games industry is small (Melbourne has the biggest indie cluster: Mighty Kingdom, Halfbrick(Brisbane), Big Ant, Hipster Whale etc. - general knowledge, not verified) and entry-level roles are scarce; Australia's biggest advantage is English-medium, 3-yr degree, 485 work rights, easier path to English-speaking global jobs.
