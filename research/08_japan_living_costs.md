# Japan (Osaka / Ritsumeikan OIC) living costs
Sources: https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/lifecareer/dorm/oic2/ (OIC Global House), https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/lifecareer/cost-of-living/ , https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/file.jsp?id=426079&version=English
Accessed 2026-10-24. Confidence: high (official), but dorm placement for non-GLA colleges is "depending on availability" (ISSE students may not be guaranteed a place -> TODO check ISSE dorm policy; the Wakebayashi/OIC dorm doc says 'students in all other colleges: April enrollees must leave by end of Feb of year after move-in' => only ~1 year in dorm).

- OIC Global House: rent 65,000 + utilities/internet 7,200 + bedding 2,000 = 74,200 JPY/month (from Mar 2026). Plus one-month entrance fee, 10,000 cleaning, admin fee, fire insurance. Private room, shared kitchen.
- Private apartment: rent 30,000-60,000; utilities 10,000; internet 4,000; food 30,000-50,000; National Health Insurance ~2,000 (as listed); phone 4,000; local transport 12,000.
- University's guide: ~117,000-167,000 JPY/month total (~1.4-2.0M JPY/yr).
- Part-time work: students on a "Student" visa may work up to 28 h/week with permission (general knowledge; ~1,100-1,300 JPY/hr in Kansai) -> could offset ~50-80k JPY/month. VERIFY. Japanese ability improves job options.
- National pension required if 20+ (student exemption possible - verify).
