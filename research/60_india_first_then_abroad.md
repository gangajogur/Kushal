## Assessment (my judgement)
- India-first total cost: B.Tech Rs 6-16 lakh (living at home in Solapur cheapest) + Master's Rs 37-48 lakh (Germany, no scholarship; Japan MEXT could be ~free) => ~Rs 45-65 lakh, 6 years, abroad from ~2031, working abroad ~2033. Aizu direct = Rs 47-56 lakh, 4 years, working ~2031. So India-first is NOT cheaper than Aizu unless he wins a scholarship; it is cheaper than ISSE without a big reduction.
- India-first wins when: (a) he gets a strong seat (NIT/IIIT/COEP/PICT/VJTI/WCE Sangli) so peers/brand/Master's pipeline are strong; (b) the family wants to cap debt and keep him near home for the first stage; (c) he wants to pursue MEXT/DAAD funding. It loses on time, on his "no Master's" preference, and on weak-tier colleges where Master's admission is less likely.
- Pick a 4-YEAR B.Tech/B.E. (not 3-year BSc/BCA) so German/Japanese Master's and MEXT accept it; keep CGPA >= 8/10 (comfortably above the 70% screens), build a research/thesis project, IELTS 7, APS, Japanese N3+.
