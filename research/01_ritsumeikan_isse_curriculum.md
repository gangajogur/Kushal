# Ritsumeikan ISSE (Information Systems Science and Engineering Course), College of Information Science and Engineering
Source: https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/ise/isse/academics/curriculum.html/?version=English
Accessed: 2026-10-24. Confidence: high for what's stated, but the page is MARKETING-LEVEL only (no course list).

## Structure (from page)
- 4 years. Year 1: basics of information science broadly; in 2nd half of year 1 choose a specialised course by interest/goals.
- Lab (research group) chosen in 1st half of year 3; from 2nd half of year 3 -> graduation research for ~1.5 years (so graduation thesis is compulsory).
- "Project-based learning from year 1." Years 1-2: product life-cycle processes + teamwork. Year 3: launch own projects leading into graduation thesis.
- "Customizable education": mix of classic science fundamentals + interdisciplinary practical courses.
- Global opportunities: extracurricular programs, internships, academic mobility.
- Page explicitly says the research approach prepares students for graduate school ("start at a deeper level when they proceed to graduate school").
