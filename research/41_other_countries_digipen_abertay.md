## DigiPen BS CS in Real-Time Interactive Simulation (RTIS), Redmond USA (also Singapore, Bilbao)
- 4 years, ABET accredited, game-programming-specific. C/C++ from year 1; Modern C++ Design Patterns; Operating Systems; Data Structures; Discrete Maths; Calculus I-II; Linear Algebra; Computer Graphics; Real-time Rendering; Networks; Motion Dynamics; ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR GAMES; Spatial Data Structures; Algorithm Analysis; Probability & Statistics; Low-Level Programming; Programming Massively Parallel Processors (GPU); Machine Learning; Mobile & Cloud; a software-engineering GAME PROJECT every semester (SEP1-6) + capstone; optional specialisations: AI, Physics simulation, Image processing, Databases/Networks, ML, Sound, DSP.
- => the MOST ON-TARGET curriculum for "systems-level gameplay/AI programmer" (this is the benchmark). Singapore campus sample sequence uses 11 trimesters / 240 credits and an "Integrated Work Study Programme" (paid-ish work placement).
- Cost (USA, international, 2026-27): tuition USD 45,500/yr, est. cost of attendance USD 74,468/yr (USD 71,370 prior year) => ~USD 298k for 4 years (at Rs 94.94/USD = ~Rs 2.8 crore). OPT/STEM OPT work route possible but H-1B lottery risk. Not a bang-for-buck pick; benchmark only (possibly Singapore/Bilbao campuses cheaper - not researched).

## Abertay University BSc (Hons) Computer Games Technology, Dundee UK
- 4-year (Scottish honours), TIGA + BCS accredited. International tuition GBP 17,950/yr (2026 entry) with an automatic international scholarship of GBP 12,000-28,000 over the course (so net ~GBP 14-16k/yr). 
- Core modules: Programming with C++; Intro to Game Development; Computer Hardware Architecture & Operating Systems; Games Programming; Applied Maths 1/2/4; Data Structures & Algorithms 1 and 2; Graphics Programming; Game Programming & System Architectures; ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (CMP304: rule-based, FSMs, behaviour trees, fuzzy logic, genetic algorithms, reinforcement learning, probabilistic techniques, ANNs, clustering, ML evaluation); Game Engine Development; Graphics Programming with Shaders; Games Networking; Professional Project; 60-credit group project (3rd yr); Honours Project + dissertation.
- Fit: curriculum is almost the same shape as DigiPen at a fraction of the US cost; the AI module literally covers game-AI + RL. UK Graduate Route visa gives 2 years post-study work (3 years for PhD) - verify current rules. Living costs Dundee ~GBP 10-12k/yr (not verified). Total approx GBP 94-110k over 4 years (tuition net of scholarship 44-60k + living ~44k + visa/IHS/flights ~6k) = ~Rs 119-140 lakh at 1 GBP = Rs 127.13 - MORE than ISSE (Rs 68-91 lakh), Aizu, Germany; LESS than Deakin/UWA/DigiPen; A Level/IB equivalence: needs India HSC 75-85% typical (verify). Starts September (intake Sept 2027 feasible - UCAS deadline 30 Jun international).
