## Ranking
1. h_da Animation & Game, TECHNOLOGY (Dieburg/Darmstadt). Fit 75%. No tuition in Hesse; semester contribution EUR 383. B2 English (IELTS 5.5). Entrance exam: portfolio of 3 work samples then on-campus/online exam, minimum 36 points. uni-assist deadline 15 Apr and exam application 1 Jun (2026/27 cycle; 2027 dates not yet published). If uni-assist gives "indirect entitlement" (likely for HSC only - VERIFY) he must pass the exam AND then Studienkolleg (entrance test in German + subject). 18-week placement/study-abroad semester 4. AI & Behaviour Programming mandatory.
2. DIT Deggendorf, B.Sc. Artificial Intelligence. Fit 60% (best ML/AI). EUR 500/sem. Apply 15 Apr-15 Jun. B2 English; German A2 by end of studies. No aptitude test. 30-ECTS internship. No graphics/game. (THI Ingolstadt CS&AI is a near-equal substitute: multi-agent/dialogue agents, EUR 800/sem, apply 1 Apr-15 Jul 2027.)
3. TH Koeln Digital Games - Game Programming (Cologne Game Lab). Fit 60%. No tuition, EUR 353/sem. Apply 1-31 Mar. ~35 seats across 3 tracks, aptitude test, B.A.; maths and AI thin; Cologne city/Gamescom; sem 5 = exchange/internship/own project.

Rejected: Constructor (private, Rs 96-119 lakh, general CS), OTH Regensburg (general CS, not module-checked), Germany's other uni CS degrees (German-taught).
