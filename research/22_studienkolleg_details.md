# Studienkolleg (T-Kurs) details for an Indian HSC student
Sources: https://www.studienkolleg.org/en/blog/studienkolleg-guide-indian-students-complete/ (guide, verified 4 Sep 2026; aggregator = medium confidence) ; TUM https://www.tum.de/en/studies/application/other-forms-of-study/preparatory-study-at-the-studienkolleg (official) ; Studienkolleg Duesseldorf T-Course https://studienkolleg-duesseldorf.de/course/t-course/?lang=en ; Studienzentrum Thueringen (Jena) https://www.studienzentrum-thueringen.de/studienangebot/t-kurs ; APS leaflet.
Accessed 2026-10-24.

- Course for CS = T-Kurs (technical/maths/natural sciences). 2 semesters (~38 weeks, ~36 class hours/week). Subjects: German (largest block, 18 of 36 teaching units at Duesseldorf), Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry; Jena also Informatics + English. Final exam = Feststellungsprüfung (FSP): written German, Maths, Physics (or Chemistry), oral Chemistry (Duesseldorf).
- Entry: Class XII certificate (he has 82%; rule >=70%), APS certificate (Rs 18,000), German B1 (Duesseldorf accepts participation confirmation; Jena B1) - B2 required at Munich (TUM/Bavaria: must show B2). Aggregator says B1 most common, B2 recommended. Some Studienkollegs need an entrance exam (e.g. TU Berlin, TU Freiberg: German + maths); Duesseldorf has none.
- Applying: via uni-assist/TUMonline/Studienkolleg directly. Munich/Bavarian: winter semester apply 15 May-15 Jul, summer 15 Nov-15 Jan. Duesseldorf start dates: 1 Feb 2027 and 1 Sep 2027 (12-month courses). Jena: WS deadline 15 Sep (rolling), SS 30 Mar.
- Fees vary hugely: public Studienkolleg (TU Berlin: free apart from semester contribution; Bavarian public: low), private/affiliated: Duesseldorf EUR 495 registration + EUR 5,995 per year; Jena EUR 8,000. Total first-year all-in per aggregator ~Rs 12-15 lakh incl. living and blocked account (EUR 11,904, which is usable for living).
- Time: whole process 6-12 months before start (language B1 4-8 months of intensive course + APS + visa). Visa needs "Studienbewerber" proof (EUR 1,091/month net for 'applicants for entrance exam' per diplo.de table vs 992 for students) - so blocked account higher for the Studienkolleg stage: ~EUR 13,092/yr (INFERENCE from diplo.de list; verify).
- After FSP: subject-restricted entry to Uni/HAW for T subjects (CS OK). FSP success only makes him eligible; places at popular programmes depend on grade quota (Studienkolleg graduates often have a quota; DAAD says quota system). Programme with aptitude test (TH Koeln Digital Games, h_da) still require the test.
- German at B1/B2 is the key bottleneck: Goethe intensive 5 days x 4 h reaches B1 in ~11 months from zero (example in guide). He has already studied German (level unknown -> ASK).
- Earliest realistic: Studienkolleg Sep/Oct 2027 (if B1 by mid-2027) -> FSP mid-2028 -> Bachelor WS 2028/29 -> graduate ~2032 (same as ISSE April 2028 start; but 3.5 yrs after).
