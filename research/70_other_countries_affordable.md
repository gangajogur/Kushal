# Other affordable countries (user question 2026-10-24) - first pass
FX used: 1 EUR = Rs 106.45; 1 USD = Rs 94.94; 1 TWD ~ Rs 2.90 (36.7 TWD/EUR per Mastersportal); 1 KRW ~ Rs 0.068 (ASSUMPTION ~1,400 KRW/USD - not verified); HUF 400/EUR approx. Estimates only. Module-level curriculum NOT yet checked for any of these; scores not given.

## 1. South Korea - KAIST (Daejeon), English-medium campus. HIGH VALUE, HIGH COMPETITION
Sources: https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-undergraduate/application/ApplicationGuide/ApplicationTimeline ; https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-undergraduate/support/scholarships/kaist ; https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-undergraduate/about/why ; https://studypath.cc/universities/kaist (cost table).
- Every admitted international undergraduate gets the KAIST Scholarship automatically (no separate application): full tuition for 8 semesters + KRW 350,000/month living support + medical insurance; keep GPA > 2.7/4.3 after freshman year. English is the official campus language; Korean not required for graduation. Freshmen are undeclared; major (incl. Computer Science) chosen after year 1, no quota per department.
- Dates (Fall 2027 entry): Early track closed 22 Oct 2026. REGULAR track: apply 10 Nov 2026 - 14 Jan 2027 (6pm KST); recommendation letter by 21 Jan; interviews (if any) 3 Mar 2027; results 25 Mar 2027; reply before August; term starts end of Aug 2027. Must submit one standardized test (SAT/ACT/AP/IB/A-level) + English test (TOEFL iBT 83 / IELTS 6.5 / TOEIC 720 / TEPS recommended; whether Duolingo/DET is accepted NOT verified).
- Cost: KAIST table lists essential non-tuition costs KRW 8.02M/yr (food 5.475M, dorm 1.328M, health insurance 0.917M, books 0.3M) vs stipend KRW 4.2M/yr => net ~KRW 3.8M/yr (~Rs 2.6 lakh) x 4 = ~Rs 10 lakh + flights/visa/laptop/personal/other ~Rs 4-8 lakh => roughly Rs 15-25 lakh total (FX and personal spending assumptions flagged).
- Risk: very selective (SAT 1310 is likely below typical admits - not verified); no guarantee of CS major; Korean game industry exists (Nexon/NCSoft/Krafton - general knowledge). Similar full-scholarship programmes at DGIST/GIST/UNIST/POSTECH exist (general knowledge, NOT verified).
- GKS-U government scholarship (embassy track) for 2027 closed 30 Sep 2026 (https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/in-en/brd/m_22090/view.do?seq=761569); covers tuition (+ KRW 14.4M/yr allowance) but needs 1 year of Korean language first (exempt with TOPIK 5-6). Next cycle: Sep 2027 for 2028 entry.

## 2. Hungary - Stipendium Hungaricum (SH) scholarship, ELTE Budapest BSc Computer Science (English). VERY CHEAP IF WON
Sources: https://apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu/courses/course/444-bsc-computer-science ; https://uni-obuda.hu/stipendium-hungaricum/ ; https://www.tok.elte.hu/en/stipendium-hungaricum-scholarship
- India is an eligible country. Covers full tuition, monthly allowance (HUF 43,700; the ELTE page's EUR figure looks mis-typed), free dorm place or HUF 40,000/month housing contribution, health insurance. 2027/28 call opens NOVEMBER 2026 on the SH site; last cycle's deadline was 15 Jan 2026 (so expect mid-Jan 2027 - verify). Preparatory year available. English BSc in CS: 6 semesters (180 ECTS), English B2, motivation letter, good school marks esp. Maths. SH holders on English programmes must also take Hungarian as a foreign language.
- Cost if won: living in Budapest ~EUR 500-800/month (ASSUMPTION) minus allowance/dorm => gap ~EUR 400-600/month x 36 months => ~Rs 15-23 lakh + flights/other => ~Rs 17-26 lakh. Selection competitive; India's slot count unknown; Hungarian degree = EU degree; Hungarian job market/permit path not researched.

## 3. Czech Republic - CTU Prague (FIT) Informatics / Charles University CS (English bachelors)
Sources: https://fit.cvut.cz/en/applicants/admissions-procedure/bachelor-study-program ; https://studyinprague.cz/wp-content/uploads/Study-in-Prague-one-sheet-2025.indd_.pdf ; https://portal.cvut.cz/informace-pro-studenty/obecne-informace/zivotni-naklady/ ; https://www.studyu.org/en/blog-cost-of-studying-in-czechia
- CTU Informatics (English): CZK 64,000/semester (~EUR 2,600) = ~EUR 5,200/yr; 3-year bachelor; apply by 31 MAR 2027 (tuition due by 15 Sep 2027). Charles University CS (English) EUR 7,100/yr non-EU, 3 years. CTU's own living estimate ~EUR 600/month (dorm EUR 150, food 220, personal 200); Study in Czechia guideline EUR 500-800.
- Cost (no scholarship): tuition EUR 15.6k (Rs 16.6 lakh) + living EUR 600 x 36 = 21.6k (Rs 23 lakh) + other ~EUR 3k (Rs 3.2 lakh) => ~Rs 43 lakh (range Rs 40-50). 3 years only. Entrance exam / recognition of Indian HSC NOT verified. No game-specific degree (CS only).

## 4. Turkey - Turkiye Burslari (government scholarship)
Sources: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/fulltimeprograms ; https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/ScholarshipSearch ; https://forsaedu.com/en/opportunities/16-turkiye-scholarships-for-bachelor-in-computer-science-software-engineering
- Apply 10 Jan - 20 Feb 2027 (TBBS portal, free), under 21 years old, >= 70% in Class 12 (engineering/IT), interview. Covers tuition, accommodation, health insurance, flights (start and end), monthly stipend TL 6,500 (undergraduate, per official page). ALL awardees take a 1-year Turkish language course first, even for English programmes (4-5 year total). Some universities offer English Computer Engineering (e.g., Abdullah Gul University, Kayseri: SAT 1100 or TOEFL iBT 75 listed; page also lists TOEFL 87 for other programmes). ~5,000 awards from 165,000 applications (aggregator) => ~3% selectivity.
- Cost if won: very low (roughly Rs 5-12 lakh, ROUGH, rate/inflation risk, personal spending). Quality varies by assigned university; placement is made by the committee; not researched for game relevance.

## 5. Poland - PJAIT (Warsaw) / Warsaw University of Technology (English CS)
Sources: https://pja.edu.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/tuition-fees-2026_2027-1.pdf ; https://www.students.pw.edu.pl/Studies-Offer/B.Sc.-offer
- PJAIT Computer Science (English, 7 semesters): PLN 24,000/yr (~EUR 5.6k); careers list includes game developer/XR/AI engineer; Polish-Japanese Academy (Japanese culture programme). WUT Computer Science (English, 8 semesters): EUR 5,520/semester = EUR 11,040/yr for non-EU. Gdansk Tech has no English CS bachelor.
- Cost estimate: PJAIT tuition EUR 19.7k (Rs 21 lakh) + living in Warsaw ~EUR 800-1,000 x 42 months (ASSUMPTION unverified) => total ~Rs 60-69 lakh = about the same as Germany (saves the Studienkolleg year only). Not a cost winner.

## 6. Taiwan
Sources: https://www.mastersportal.com/articles/2899/tuition-fees-and-living-costs-in-taiwan.html ; NTU fee sheet ; https://apply.nthu.edu.tw/en/article/102-nthu-scholarship
- NTU international undergraduate (EE & CS college) ~TWD 61,240/semester (~Rs 3.6 lakh/yr); NTHU EECS TWD 53,200/semester; NTHU scholarship = tuition waived + TWD 5,000/month for bachelor students; living TWD 20-35k/month (EUR 545-955). Rough 4-yr cost Rs 46-66 lakh without a waiver, Rs 25-45 lakh with the NTHU-type waiver. English-taught CS bachelor availability NOT verified (undergraduate teaching is mostly Mandarin) => low priority unless an English bachelor is confirmed.
