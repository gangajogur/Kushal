# Part-time work: Germany vs Japan (and what it shaves off the budget) - 2026-10-24
FX: 1 EUR = Rs 106.45; 100 JPY = Rs 60.49. Estimates, not quotes. Hours/wage scenarios are MY assumptions on top of official rules.

## Rules (official / high confidence)
GERMANY (non-EU student residence permit, 16b AufenthG https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html ; DAAD https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/work-career/side-jobs/ ; Dresden ABH):
- 140 full days or 280 half days per calendar year (a day <=4 h counts half). Weekly alternative: up to 20 h/week in term counts as 2.5 days/week (outside term any hours = 2.5 days) => 140 days ~ 56 weeks, so the cap is NOT binding at <=20 h/week. Student-assistant (HiWi/academic) jobs and compulsory internships do NOT count (no limit). Freelance/self-employment not allowed without permission. (Older diplo.de pages still say 120/240 days - the statute says 140/280.)
- Studienkolleg: diplo.de says Studienkolleg students may not work in their first year of residence except during holidays (statute treats Studienkolleg as study-preparatory under the same permit - VERIFY with the Auslaenderbehoerde).
- Minimum wage EUR 13.90/h since Jan 2026. Minijob limit EUR 603/month (EUR 7,236/yr) - no income tax; above that, tax/social contributions apply (tax refundable via return). Working >20 h/week in term triggers social insurance and counts against studies.
JAPAN (ISA https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/procedures/nyuukokukanri07_00045.html ; Aizu page https://u-aizu.ac.jp/en/campus/residencecard/working.html ):
- Need 'permission to engage in activity other than that permitted' (comprehensive permission, easy to get on arrival). Up to 28 h/week across ALL jobs; during official long school vacations up to 8 h/day and 40 h/week. Breach can mean refusal of visa renewal/deportation. University SA/TA/RA jobs also count toward 28 h (no separate permit).
- Minimum wages: Osaka JPY 1,177 (since 16 Oct 2025), JPY 1,231 from 1 Oct 2026 (Path2Japan summary of MHLW). Fukushima Prefecture (Aizu) JPY 1,094 from 16 Oct 2026 (+61; official MHLW/Fukushima).
- Hourly pay in rupees at the legal floor: Germany ~Rs 1,480/h; Osaka ~Rs 745/h; Aizu ~Rs 660/h => Germany pays ~2x per hour (but living costs are also higher).
- Language: most Japanese part-time jobs (konbini, restaurants, shops) need daily Japanese (roughly N3-N2); English-capable roles exist mainly in tourism/guesthouses/English tutoring in Osaka, rarer in Aizu town. At Aizu, SA/TA/RA jobs at the university are an English-friendly option. Germany: service jobs mostly need German; English-only jobs mostly university/HiWi/tech roles.

## Aizu cost correction (official page https://u-aizu.ac.jp/sgu/en/ictg/ictg_course/ and https://u-aizu.ac.jp/en/campus/apartment/)
Living expenses: JPY 60,000-100,000/month (housing 20-60k + food 30k + recreation 10k). Somei House (year-1 housing for privately funded international students, max 1 year): rent 19,000 + utilities 10,000 = 29,000/month + 18,000 move-out cleaning; private apartments JPY 30-50k plus deposit/key money. Entry fees JPY 655,680 (admission 564,000 + insurances + others 82,300) + tuition 520,800/yr + application fee 17,000.
=> Aizu all-in revised: tuition+fees ~JPY 2.76M (Rs 16.7 lakh) + living JPY 2.88-4.8M (mid 3.84M = Rs 23 lakh) + other 0.8M => JPY 6.4-8.4M = Rs 39-51 lakh (mid ~45). (Replaces earlier Rs 47-56 lakh; my earlier living estimate of 90-120k/month was too high.)

## Earnings scenarios over the whole degree (3.5 effective working years: Germany has a restricted Studienkolleg year; Japan year 1 assumed half effectiveness)
GERMANY at EUR 13.90 (Minijob-level):
- Low: 5 h/week x 40 weeks = 200 h/yr => EUR 2,780/yr x 3.5 + ~EUR 1,000 (Studienkolleg holidays) = ~EUR 10.7k = Rs 11.4 lakh
- Mid: 10 h/week x 40 weeks = 400 h => EUR 5,560/yr x 3.5 + 1,000 = ~EUR 20.5k = Rs 21.8 lakh (about the EUR 603/month Minijob ceiling ~43 h/month)
- High: HiWi/Werkstudent tech job ~EUR 17/h (ASSUMPTION, general knowledge, unverified) x 12 h/wk x 40 wks = EUR 8.2k/yr x 3.5 + 1,000 = ~EUR 29.6k = Rs 31.5 lakh
JAPAN, ISSE/Osaka at JPY 1,231:
- Low: 8 h/week x 40 weeks = 320 h => JPY 394k/yr x 3.5 = JPY 1.38M = Rs 8.3 lakh
- Mid: 12 h/week x 44 weeks = 528 h => JPY 650k/yr x 3.5 = JPY 2.27M = Rs 13.8 lakh
- High: 20 h/week x 44 weeks = 880 h => JPY 1.08M/yr x 3.5 = JPY 3.79M = Rs 22.9 lakh (hard alongside ISSE's project-based workload)
JAPAN, Aizu at JPY 1,094:
- Low JPY 1.23M = Rs 7.4 lakh; Mid JPY 2.02M = Rs 12.2 lakh; High JPY 3.37M = Rs 20.4 lakh (jobs scarcer, mostly Japanese-language)

## Net effect (realistic = Low to Mid)
- Germany (Rs 62-74 lakh): shave Rs 11-22 lakh => net Rs ~45-60 lakh (-17% to -30%).
- ISSE (Rs 82 lakh at 20% reduction): shave Rs 8-14 lakh => ~Rs 68-74 lakh (-10% to -17%).
- Aizu (Rs 39-51 lakh): shave Rs 7-12 lakh => ~Rs 30-40 lakh (-15% to -25%).
- Part-time income arrives month by month AFTER costs: it reduces loan size / living drawdown but cannot replace the upfront tuition, visa proof (blocked account EUR 11,904/yr in Germany) or loan principal.
- Caveats: study load (ISSE project-based; German Studienkolleg is 36 h/week), language ability, local job market (Aizu small town), exam periods, tax/insurance details unverified for Japan; Studienkolleg work ban could remove the first-year income.
