# UWA Computer Science major (MJD-CMPSC) within Bachelor of Science (3 yr)
Source: https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/courses/computer-science (fetched OK, 2026-10-24). Confidence: high for unit names; NO international fee on page.
Campus: Perth (Crawley). Full/part-time. ACS accredited (Seoul Accord). English: IELTS 6.5 (no band <6.0). Apply through TISC (WA) - international applicants via UWA direct (verify; DET acceptance TBD).
It is a MAJOR in BSc / BPhil / BSc (Integrated Professional), not a stand-alone BCS. Further study: Honours in CS, Master of Data Science, Master of IT.

## Units
Level 1 core: CITS1003 Intro to Cybersecurity; CITS1401 Computational Thinking with Python; CITS1402 Relational DB Management. (Bridging: MATH1721 Maths Foundations Methods if needed.) (Rest of Level 1 = other science units.)
Level 2 core: CITS2002 Systems Programming (C); CITS2005 Object Oriented Programming; CITS2200 Data Structures and Algorithms; CITS2211 Discrete Structures
Level 3 core: CITS3001 Advanced Algorithms; CITS3002 Computer Networks; CITS3200 Professional Computing (team project); CITS3403 Agile Web Development
Level 3 option (take 1): CITS3003 Graphics and Animation; CITS3005 Knowledge Representation; CITS3007 Secure Coding; CITS3009 CS WIL Internship; CITS3011 INTELLIGENT AGENTS; CITS3203 HCI; CITS3402 High Performance Computing
(Other UWA CS electives like Machine Learning/AI exist in other majors e.g. Data Science - NOT on this page; verify.)

## Fit vs game-AI/gameplay-systems goals
- STRONG: Systems Programming in C (memory management - matches his "memory wall" interest), DSA, Advanced Algorithms, Discrete Structures, Graphics & Animation (elective), Intelligent Agents (elective - closest to game AI/NPC), High Performance Computing (elective).
- Only ONE level-3 option => cannot take both Graphics and Intelligent Agents inside the major (could via other major/electives in BSc: verify).
- WEAK: ML/DL not visible in this major (need Data Science major as second major: "can choose second major"), no thesis, optional internship.
- Rank: UWA is a Group of Eight, top-100 globally (QS ~ top 70-80; VERIFY). Stronger brand than Deakin.
- Compared to Deakin: more systems/low-level (C), less AI/ML breadth in-major.
