My role is accommodation, pastoral support, and practical help settling into Brisbane study life. If short-term living expenses arise before loan disbursements, I am willing to assist as needed and can provide further financial evidence on request.

```
I understand he must maintain enrolment, make satisfactory course progress, hold OSHC, and comply with all Subclass 500 visa conditions.

I am happy to be contacted to confirm these arrangements:

Phone: [mobile]
Email: [email]
Address: [full address]

Yours sincerely,

[Signature]
[Full name]
Australian Citizen
[Driver licence / passport number — optional]
```

**Attach with the letter**
- Australian citizenship evidence (passport bio page or citizenship certificate)
- Proof of residence/ownership (rates notice, title search, or mortgage statement + recent utility bill) — redact account numbers if you prefer
- Relationship evidence if available (family photos not required; a short family tree note or mother’s letter naming you as brother is enough if relationship might be questioned)
- Wife’s employment letter (optional, strengthens household stability)

**Do not**
- Claim you are paying full tuition unless that becomes true and bank evidence matches
- Use wording that the only purpose of the visa is permanent residence
