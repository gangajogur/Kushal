## 1. Bottom line

1. The only way to start university before mid-2027 is overseas. India's counselling cycles all end in an August 2027 start. Australia offers February/March 2027 intakes. Choosing India means roughly 9 more months of waiting.

5. **Deakin offer already in hand (Updated, 2nd pass).** Treat it as the safe seat. Check whether the offer letter already includes the Deakin 25% scholarship (section 10.3). Deakin is in Melbourne, while QUT and Griffith are in Queensland.

## 6. Overseas-career likelihood: the evidence

Studying abroad does not by itself deliver an overseas career. The evidence I found:

- **Australia, international undergraduates in Computing and information systems (QILT GOS 2025):** 42.2% in full-time work 4–6 months after graduating, 69.3% in any work, median salary A$65,000. The 2024 figure was 43.5% [O: [QILT 2025 GOS International Report](https://qilt.edu.au/docs/default-source/default-document-library/2025-gos-international-report.pdf)]. International graduates trail domestic graduates in full-time work.
- **Australia, entry-level market (Tech Council of Australia, 2026):** software engineering employment is growing about 7% a year. But 98% of programming roles require prior experience, and the average required experience rose from 3.8 to 4.6 years. Technical workers aged 15–24 fell about 29% from the mid-2023 peak to Nov 2025. TCA found no evidence of an AI-driven early-career decline in Australian data, and says that cannot be ruled out [O: [TCA Tech Jobs Update 2026](https://techcouncil.com.au/wp-content/uploads/TCA-Tech-Jobs-Update-2026-Report-FINAL-1.pdf)].
- **UK:** computer science graduates had the highest unemployment of any subject, about 10% 15 months after graduating (2022–23 cohort), against 6.2% for all graduates (HESA, as reported by the [Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/money/jobs/schools-universities/computer-science-graduates-cant-get-job/)). UK tech job adverts fell about 50% from 2019/20 to 2024/25, with entry-level roles hit hardest ([BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm21dvg8l1go)). The 2027 Guardian University Guide found coding and software development were the fastest-falling graduate occupations ([Guardian, 12 Sep 2026](https://www.theguardian.com/education/2026/sep/12/ai-computer-science-graduates-job-prospects-uk-data)).
- **Visa runway after graduating:** Australia 2 years (485), UK 18 months for 2027 starters, Canada up to 3 years if CLB 7 is met. Each is short relative to the time needed to convert a graduate job into sponsorship or PR.
- **Indian degree then overseas Master's or work (Updated, 2nd pass):** now covered in section 10.4, with visa rules and master's fees. I still have no outcome data for Indian-degree graduates who move abroad.

[EST] Overseas study gives him local work rights, a local network and a local degree. That helps an overseas career, but the junior tech market is weak everywhere. The expected outcome is a coin-flip on a full-time graduate job within six months, not a certainty.

### 10.1 QUT scholarship: the 90% rule

**Short answer: Kushal does not qualify.** QUT's International Merit Scholarship gives 25% of tuition for the whole degree. For Indian state boards it needs a **90% average over the best 5 subjects (2 languages plus 3 academic subjects, excluding PE)**. CBSE/ISC students need only 80%. His 82% overall is well short.

- Official page with the India table: [QUT International Merit Scholarship](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/fees-and-scholarships/scholarships/international-merit-scholarship) [O], under "Minimum academic standards: undergraduate degrees and vertical masters", section "India".
- No separate application. QUT assesses it at application and states the result in the offer letter.
- If an offer arrives without it, QUT's page says to ask your agent for a review (or reply to the offer email if applied directly). Any review request needs a statement and supporting documents. I don't expect it to succeed at 82%, because the threshold is a flat number.
- Maharashtra is a QUT-recognised state board (75% minimum for admission, from Shiksha [3P], with the course threshold on [QUT BIT page](https://www.qut.edu.au/courses/bachelor-of-information-technology)). Admission and scholarship thresholds are different tests.
- QUT's own 2027 [international Year 12 guide](https://cms.qut.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/1566471/27516-Year-12-International-Guide-2027_DIGITAL_F.pdf) lists the BIT (418801) at selection rank 70, 3 years, Gardens Point.

### 10.4 Indian degree first, then a master's or graduate diploma (brief thoughts)

**Facts I could verify**

- **Graduate diploma does not work on its own.** The Australian Study Requirement for the 485 visa is 92 CRICOS weeks (two academic years) and 16 calendar months in Australia. A graduate diploma counts only if it follows, and is related to, an **Australian** bachelor's, master's or PhD completed in the same or next academic year. A diploma after an Indian degree does not meet the requirement. Graduate certificates no longer count at all. [3P migration agent summaries: [Austmss](https://www.austmss.com.au/subclass-485-graduate-diploma-changes/), [Study NT](https://studynt.nt.gov.au/news/streamlined-study-requirement-for-temporary-graduate-visas); regulation text: [legislation.gov.au F2024L00748 explanatory statement](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2024L00748/asmade/2024-06-25/es/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html)]
- **A 1.5 or 2 year coursework master's does qualify.** Under the Australia–India trade agreement, Indian nationals get up to **3 years** after a coursework master's, versus 2 years after a bachelor's (3 years for a first-class STEM honours bachelor's). [Victorian Government, [Study Melbourne](https://www.studymelbourne.vic.gov.au/news-updates/extended-post-study-work-rights)]. Confirm on Home Affairs before relying on it.
- **Master's of IT is open to any bachelor's, usually at 60%.** 2027 fees: QUT AUD 46,800/yr (2 years; 1.5 with an IT bachelor's and GPA 4.0/7) [O: [QUT MIT](https://www.qut.edu.au/courses/master-of-information-technology)]; Monash AUD 44,740/yr (2 years, or 1.5 with a qualifying IT major, 60% average) [O: [Monash MIT](https://www.monash.edu/study/courses/find-a-course/information-technology-c6001)]; Deakin AUD 45,800/yr (2 years) [O: [Deakin MIT](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/master-information-technology-international)].
- **A master's does not raise salary for international graduates in the QILT data.** International postgraduate-coursework graduates earned a median about A$69,400 in 2025, close to international undergraduates at A$70,000, and A$35,300 below domestic postgraduates [O: [QILT 2025 GOS International Report](https://qilt.edu.au/docs/default-source/default-document-library/2025-gos-international-report.pdf)]. The report says this is partly because many international master's students are young and move straight from their bachelor's.

**Cost sketch** [EST; tuition held flat, living AUD 30–35k/yr, visa AUD 2.5k]:

| Route | Cost | Years of study |
|---|---|---|
| Australian bachelor's only (QUT/Deakin/Griffith, with likely scholarships) | ₹1.27–1.65 cr | 3 |
| Indian B.Tech (BITS or MIT Manipal) | ₹0.25–0.32 cr | 4 |
| Australian 2-year master's (Monash) | ₹1.02–1.08 cr | 2 |
| Australian 1.5-year master's (Monash, with an IT bachelor's) | ₹0.77–0.82 cr | 1.5 |
| **B.Tech plus 2-year Australian master's** | **about ₹1.3–1.4 cr** | 6 |

So the total money is similar to an Australian bachelor's alone. What changes is time and what you end up with.

**My view**

- This is a respectable Plan B, but not obviously better for Kushal. It costs about the same overall and buys a good Indian degree, more maturity at master's age, and a 3-year post-study visa under the India agreement.
- It delays earning in Australia by roughly three to four years compared with a 2027 start, and it adds another round of admission and visa risk in 2031 or later. Australian visa rules have changed in 2024, 2025 and 2026, so a plan that depends on today's settings over six years is fragile.
- The Indian leg is worth it only at a strong college (BITS, NIT/IIIT, or MIT Manipal). A VIT, Amrita or SRM degree followed by an Australian master's spends the same overseas money on a weaker first credential.
- A graduate diploma is the wrong tool. It cannot deliver the 485 visa after an Indian degree. If he goes India-first and wants Australia, plan on a full coursework master's.
- Given he has already lost a year, wants an overseas career and has the budget, starting an Australian bachelor's in 2027 remains my recommendation. If the family weights cost and a stronger brand more, India-first with an Australian master's is a reasonable alternative. The two cannot both be done: starting at Deakin or QUT in March 2027 means giving up the 2027 Indian cycle.
