## 1. Bottom line

1. The only way to start university before mid-2027 is overseas. India's counselling cycles all end in an August 2027 start. Australia offers February/March 2027 intakes. Choosing India means roughly 9 more months of waiting.
2. **Recommendation for this student: study in Australia, starting Feb/Mar 2027.** Monash is the strongest realistic target, then QUT, then Deakin (offer in hand) as the safe seat. UWA ranks fifth in Australia and APU (Kuala Lumpur) is a separate low-cost option. Full Australian ranking is in section 40.1 **(Updated, 2nd and 3rd pass)**. Run the Indian applications in parallel as a fallback for August 2027. They are cheap to file (₹1,300–2,000 each).
3. The case for India is cost, not speed. A comparable BITS or MIT Manipal degree costs about ₹24–32 lakh, against about ₹1.5–1.9 crore in Australia. Overseas study does not guarantee an overseas career (section 30.2).
4. Two gating items for Australia:
   - **English test.** Duolingo is not accepted by QUT or Monash for direct entry. It is also not an approved Australian student-visa test. Book IELTS Academic or PTE Academic now.
   - **Deadlines.** QUT closes on 25 Jan 2027 for offshore Indian applicants. The UTS Autumn deadline is 30 Nov 2026.
   - **Agent rule (Updated, 2nd pass).** QUT and Deakin require offshore Indian applicants to apply through an authorised agent or representative. Griffith, Monash and Swinburne allow direct applications (section 41.2).
5. **Deakin offer already in hand (Updated, 2nd pass).** Treat it as the safe seat. Check whether the offer letter already includes the Deakin 25% scholarship (section 40.1). Deakin is in Melbourne, while QUT and Griffith are in Queensland.
6. **UWA and APU (Updated, 4th pass).** UWA costs about ₹1.7–1.8 cr, its BSc has no machine learning, and its India deadline is **28 Dec 2026** (section 60.1). APU costs about ₹41–56 lakh and starts 25 Mar 2027, but a Malaysian degree gives no post-study work route (section 61.1). Curriculum fit across Deakin, QUT, UWA and APU is in section 62.1.
