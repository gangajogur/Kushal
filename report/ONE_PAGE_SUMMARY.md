**Question:** Best bachelor's route for a nephew (India; HSC 82%, SAT 1310, DET 130) who wants to work on game AI, with cost in mind.

**Answer in one paragraph:** Ritsumeikan ISSE (Japan) is the best academic fit (game-AI professor and lab inside the English course; 81% on my skills checklist vs 49-61% for Deakin/UWA/THI), but it costs ~Rs 69-91 lakh, requires him to reach Japanese N2-N1 to work in Japanese game studios, and the April 2027 application rounds have closed (next: April 2028). Germany public is the best-value overseas option (~Rs 56-72 lakh), but needs Studienkolleg or one Indian bachelor's year first, and he disliked its culture. Australia (Rs 140-171 lakh) is the worst fit and cost for games. The lowest-regret baseline is an Indian B.Tech + portfolio, then a funded Master's abroad.
