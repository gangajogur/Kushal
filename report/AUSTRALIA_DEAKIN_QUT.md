## Assessment (my judgement)
- Academic eligibility is NOT the barrier; money and IELTS are. Australia is the abroad route he is most certain to be admitted to (with IELTS), at the highest price.
- Best Australian fit = QUT Games (Software Technologies): the only admissible-for-sure degree with graphics, engine and game-AI content, a team capstone with industry, in the user's city (family base; possible big cost cut). Weakness: unverified C++/systems depth; the games industry is small and layoffs are heavy (GDC 2026); AI-era entry-level squeeze.
- Worth pursuing only if the family can fund ~Rs 1.2-1.5 crore without distress; otherwise India-first (+ Master's/work abroad) remains better value. Deakin is dearer in practice (Melbourne, no family) despite the 20% merit scholarship.
- Timeline pressure: QUT Feb 2027 requires application by 25 Jan 2027 and IELTS/PTE/TOEFL before that.
