## 90.3 Master's abroad after an Indian B.Tech (India-first route)

India-first total, from the earlier note: B.Tech ₹6–16 lakh (cheapest living at home) plus a master's ₹37–48 lakh (Germany) gives about **₹45–65 lakh over about 6 years**, with working abroad from about 2033. [EST]

- **Germany master's:** typically needs CGPA of about 70% (7 of 10), IELTS 7.0, APS certificate, and the Indian college must be rated H+ on anabin (a German database). Whether a given college is H+ was not checked.
- **MEXT research student (Japan):** at least 70% in a relevant bachelor's, born on or after 2 Apr 1992 (changes yearly), degree by 30 Sep of the entry year. CS/IT is an eligible field. Tuition exempt, stipend about JPY 144,000 a month for regular master's students. Applications about May. Competitive.
- **Australia, US:** a master's after an Indian degree is covered in `50_india_then_master.md`.
