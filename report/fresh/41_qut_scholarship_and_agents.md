## 41.1 QUT scholarship: the 90% rule

**Short answer: Kushal does not qualify.** QUT's International Merit Scholarship gives 25% of tuition for the whole degree. For Indian state boards it needs a **90% average over the best 5 subjects (2 languages plus 3 academic subjects, excluding PE)**. CBSE/ISC students need only 80%. His 82% overall is well short.

- Official page with the India table: [QUT International Merit Scholarship](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/fees-and-scholarships/scholarships/international-merit-scholarship) [O], under "Minimum academic standards: undergraduate degrees and vertical masters", section "India".
- No separate application. QUT assesses it at application and states the result in the offer letter.
- If an offer arrives without it, QUT's page says to ask your agent for a review (or reply to the offer email if applied directly). Any review request needs a statement and supporting documents. I don't expect it to succeed at 82%, because the threshold is a flat number.
- Maharashtra is a QUT-recognised state board (75% minimum for admission, from Shiksha [3P], with the course threshold on [QUT BIT page](https://www.qut.edu.au/courses/bachelor-of-information-technology)). Admission and scholarship thresholds are different tests.
- QUT's own 2027 [international Year 12 guide](https://cms.qut.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/1566471/27516-Year-12-International-Guide-2027_DIGITAL_F.pdf) lists the BIT (418801) at selection rank 70, 3 years, Gardens Point.

## 41.2 Direct application or agent?

It depends on the university. The rule comes from the student-visa risk framework, so it is a university policy and not a choice for the family.

| University | Can Kushal apply directly from India? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **QUT** | **No.** Offshore applicants from India must apply through a QUT official representative. The application portal blocks direct submission, and a South Asia Genuine Student form is added. | [QUT list of countries needing a representative](https://cms.qut.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0004/639526/genuine-students-representative-requirements.pdf), [QUT advising students](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/international/for-agents/advising-students) [O] |
| **Deakin** | **No.** India is on the list of countries that must apply through an authorised agent. | [Deakin authorised agents](https://www.deakin.edu.au/international-students/help-or-contact/support-in-your-country-or-region/authorised-international-agents) [O] |
| **Griffith** | **Yes.** Apply online or through a registered agent. Griffith's South Asia page lists a "Direct applicants" contact. | [Griffith international applications](https://www.griffith.edu.au/apply/international), [Griffith South Asia page](https://www.griffith.edu.au/international/contact-us/admissions/south-asia-middle-east) [O] |
| **Monash** | **Yes.** Indian applicants apply through Monash's online portal. An agent is optional, and must be on Monash's authorised list. | [Monash India FAQ](https://www.monash.edu/india/study/frequently-asked-questions), [applying through an agent](https://www.monash.edu/study/how-to-apply/application-process/international-students/applying-through-an-education-agent) [O] |
| **Swinburne** | **Yes.** "Apply directly or find a Swinburne-authorised education agent." | [Swinburne India page](https://www.swinburne.edu.au/international-students/countries/india/) [O] |
| RMIT, Macquarie | Not checked this pass. Confirm on each university's India page. | |

I assume Kushal is applying from India. If he is already in Australia on a valid visa, QUT's "onshore" rules differ (the onshore deadline for July 2027 is 5 Jul 2027). Check with QUT International.

**How agents are paid.** Griffith's agent page says it pays commission to registered agents per eligible student. So the service is usually free to the student, and the agent still has an incentive to steer you toward universities it is contracted with. Monash warns that some agents charge for extra services and to be wary of agents who claim to represent Monash when they don't. [O]
