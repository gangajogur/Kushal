**Honest reading.** QUT and Deakin are close in rankings (160 vs 172), and Griffith is clearly lower on the QS subject table. On QILT numbers, none of the three is clearly better for an international CS graduate, because those figures are all-field, provider-level and not course-level. Do not choose between them on those numbers. The large difference is cost: with the scholarships likely to apply, Deakin and Griffith are about ₹27 lakh cheaper than QUT. The other difference is location.

Ranked order for the three (my judgement): **1. QUT, 2. Deakin, 3. Griffith.** QUT leads only because of the Brisbane location and slightly better ranking. It is the most expensive of the three, with no scholarship for him. If the family is comfortable at ₹1.6 cr, QUT is the best of the three. If cost matters more, or he does not need family nearby, **Deakin overtakes QUT** and is the better value. Griffith is the cheapest Queensland option, with the lowest academic ranking.

**Practical advice**

- **Do not let the Deakin offer lapse.** Check the acceptance deadline and whether the deposit is refundable before paying. Accepting a seat commits money and visa time; keeping QUT/Monash applications alive costs only application fees.
- **Intake sequencing.** Deakin offers T1 (March), T2 (July) and T3 (November). If you want Indian exam results (MET, BITSAT) before committing, the Deakin T2 July 2027 intake is the natural fallback. It costs about four months.
- **Accreditation and skills assessment.** QUT states its BIT is ACS-accredited. I did not check the other degrees (relevant to later sponsorship or PR). Ask each university and agent.

## 40.2 Additional Australia notes (carried over, 5th pass)

*Carried over from an earlier Australia note dated 1 Nov 2026 (removed from the repo; git commit `a643bc8`). Some figures use older exchange rates and are estimates.*

- **Living with family in Brisbane changes the QUT cost a lot.** The earlier note assumed living costs of about AUD 12k a year when living with family, against about AUD 28k renting. Applying that to QUT BIT: tuition AUD 140.1k + living AUD 36k + other costs about AUD 8k = about AUD 184k, which is **about ₹1.23 cr** [EST; the AUD 12k a year is an assumption not confirmed with the family]. The cost tables in 20.1 and 40.1 assume he rents (AUD 30–35k a year), so they overstate QUT if he lives with you. The `Loan/` files assume board is covered by the uncle in Brisbane. Deakin (Melbourne) and UWA (Perth) get no such saving.
- **QUT Diploma in Information Technology pathway.** AUD 28,200, 8–12 months, entry Feb, Jul or Nov. Gives one year of credit into the Bachelor of IT or the Games degree. Tuition would be about AUD 28.2k + 2 × AUD 46.2k = about AUD 120.6k, against AUD 138.6k direct. Credit details unverified. Fee figures here are from the old note: AUD 46,200 for the Games degree, against AUD 44,300 in `62_curriculum_fit.md` (older figure). Confirm.
- **Visa capacity.** National Planning Level for 2027 is 295,000 (not a hard cap). Allocations: QUT 4,750, Deakin 6,550. Visa processing slows once a university passes about 80% of its allocation [3P/O: studyaustralia.gov.au news and education.gov.au allocations PDF]. This is a reason to apply early.
- **Work rights.** Students could work 48 hours a fortnight in session and unlimited hours in scheduled breaks (verify; rules changed in 2023–24). From 2 Oct 2026 partners and children generally can't be added to a student visa.
- **Games industry in Australia is small** (a Melbourne indie cluster; Brisbane has some studios), and entry-level roles are scarce. Australia's advantage for him is English-medium teaching, a 3-year degree, the post-study work visa and an easier path to English-speaking jobs. See `../INTERESTS_WORTH_PURSUING.md`.
