## 41.1 HSC history: what it changes

I don't know whether Kushal *passed* in 2025 or only *appeared* in 2025. The two cases differ.

| Exam | Scenario A: passed 2025, improved 2026 | Scenario B: failed or only appeared 2025, first pass 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| **JEE Main 2027** [O: NTA bulletin] | Eligible (passed 2025 or 2026). The "passing year" is the first year declared pass, and improvement attempts don't change it. 2027 would be his **last** eligible year. | Eligible in 2027 and 2028. |
| **JoSAA / NIT / IIIT** (needs 75% or top-20 percentile) | 2026 rules use the higher of the 2025 and 2026 marks per subject for the 75% route. His 82% clears it. **2027 rules are not published**, so confirm. | Same. 82% clears 75%. |
| **BITSAT-2027** | **Likely not eligible** [UNVERIFIED]. The 2026 brochure says "first appeared in Class 12 in 2025 or 2026". The 2027 brochure is not out (opens about Dec 2026). | **Unclear.** BITS's wording mixes "first appeared" with "passed in previous/current year". Ask BITS in writing. |
| **MET (Manipal), AEEE (Amrita), SRMJEEE, MHT-CET** | No repeat-year restriction seen on official pages. | Same. |
| **VITEEE 2027** | Eligible (passed 2025/2026). Some clauses about multiple board sittings are ambiguous, so get written confirmation. | Eligible. |
| **Overseas universities** | They assess the Class 12 certificate you present. Always disclose both sittings. | Same. |

Sources: [JEE Main 2027 bulletin](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3f8e59f4b2fe7c5705bf878bbd494ccdf/uploads/2026/10/202610241890224107.pdf), the JoSAA 2026 business rules (Annexure 2(b)) and the BITSAT-2026 brochure.

### Canada (Fall 2027 start; 4-year degrees; no speed advantage)

- **Top schools are out of reach.** Waterloo CS needs 85% overall with 90% in Maths. U of T recommends "low 90s".
- **Mid-tier schools are plausible** [3P/unverified]: TMU (65% average), Dalhousie (70% overall, Maths 75%), SFU (50% in Maths, but CS is competitive).
- **Study permit.** A provincial attestation letter (PAL) is required. 2027 allocations are **not yet published**, and a 2026 PAL can't be reused.
- **Funds.** Proof of funds is CAD 23,448 plus tuition and travel.
- **Post-study work.** A PGWP needs CLB 7 in all four skills on IELTS General Training, CELPIP General or PTE Core. IELTS Academic and Duolingo don't qualify.
- **PR.** Canadian Experience Class cutoffs in 2026 ran about 507–523 CRS, and software developers were not a 2026 category.
